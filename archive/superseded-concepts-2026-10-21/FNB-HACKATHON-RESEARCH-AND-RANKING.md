# FNB App of the Year: evidence, opportunities and ranked concepts

Research date: 5 October 2026. Scope: the six official problem statements confirmed in the user's screenshots and on the [hackathon page](https://appoftheyear.co.za/hackathon/).

## Recommendation

Build **PaperBridge**, an offline application assistant that sees the exact document issue blocking a learner, explains the next step in their language, and prepares a checked packet for official submission. Lead with one education funding workflow. Add an affordable study-path calculator only after that workflow works.

**ProofShift** is the strongest alternative if an employer will define and review a practical assessment. **ScamPause** is the strongest compact financial demo. **ColdLoop** is the ambitious physical-world wildcard if you already have access to cold-storage operators and sensors.

No concept guarantees a prize. The recommendation balances evidence of a specific solvable problem, originality, usable demonstration, and a credible route to adoption. It is a research judgment, not a prediction of judges' decisions or an exhaustive novelty search.

Planning assumption: 3–5 people and a 72-hour build. The team size was not provided. A solo builder should retain one workflow and one reliable input method. The [organiser's FAQ](https://appoftheyear.co.za/faqs/) describes product quality, creativity, usability, sustainability, impact, presentation and intelligence for the awards. It is useful guidance, but it does not establish the hackathon's exact 2026 scoring weights or preparation rules.

## 2. The evidence that most changes the recommendation

**Education has a documented administrative bottleneck.** On 2 January 2026, NSFAS recorded 218,043 incomplete first-time applications awaiting documents out of 893,847: about 24.4%, calculated from the statement. This is a historical processing snapshot, not today's backlog or the proportion permanently rejected. [Official January statement](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/nsfas-concludes-all-funding-decisions-2026-and-mop-payments-2025-02-jan-2026)

By 5 February, NSFAS said more than 180,000 outstanding documents had been submitted, contributing to 50,000 additional approvals. It identified missing dates, incorrect signers, incomplete consent information and unclear documents. Its table then showed 21,483 applications awaiting documents and 85,662 in verification. These states and dates must remain separate. [Official February statement](https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/student-financial-aid-scheme-status-applications-2026-academic-year-05-feb-2026)

This supports testing document assistance. It does not prove all incomplete cases were preventable, that an app caused approvals, or that forms are the biggest education barrier overall.

**Credible skills information has experimental support.** A Johannesburg randomized study found publicly shareable assessment results improved employment by about five percentage points and weekly earnings by 34%. The intervention involved established assessments and credible institutional branding. This supports the information mechanism behind ProofShift, but does not validate AI-generated credentials or forecast our impact. [Research team's Oxford summary](https://mbrg.bsg.ox.ac.uk/mind-and-behaviour-projects/impact-providing-information-about-job-seekers-skills-employment-south); [original AER paper](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20200961)

A separate Soweto experiment found transport support increased search without increasing employment overall. Affordability is still important, but a travel feature needs real hiring demand behind it. [J-PAL study](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/evaluation/transport-subsidies-and-job-matchmaking-south-africa)

## 4. Ranking method

Scores below are subjective 1–5 assessments, with 5 strongest. They are not measured product performance, market size, or probabilities of winning. Assume a 72-hour build without committed external partners. Adjacent ranks are uncertain.

Weights: need and supporting evidence 20%; how directly the app changes the bottleneck 25%; distinctiveness 20%; demonstration 15%; build feasibility 15%; adoption/revenue path 5%. Weighted total = sum(weight × score) / 5.

CareRelay has a more severe human need; PayRhythm is easier to prototype. ColdLoop rises with a hardware/operator partner. ProofShift can become the first choice with an employer-backed trial. PaperBridge's high feasibility applies to one known form family, not arbitrary government documents.

## 5. Ranked concepts

### 1 — PaperBridge: turn a blocked application into a completed next step

**Problem statement:** Learners navigating education funding can be delayed by incomplete evidence, unclear requirements and documents that depend on one another. How can a learner identify and resolve the next preventable blocker, in their own language and with intermittent connectivity?

**The product:** Point the camera at a supported form. The app identifies its version, checks legibility and mandatory fields, and highlights the exact issue on the page. The learner hears a short explanation and sees who needs to act. Correcting the evidence updates an application dependency graph and produces a reviewable packet.

The fresh part is a small **application rules engine**: requirement → source → evidence → responsible person → next step. A scanner alone digitises an error; this workflow helps someone resolve it. It handles an unknown requirement by asking for review, rather than guessing. “Present” and “authentic” remain separate checks.

**Demo:** Put the phone in airplane mode. Scan a synthetic consent form with a blank date and an empty required signature box. Highlight both locations, play a reviewed isiZulu explanation, and show why the application cannot yet be marked ready. Add a properly completed sample, rerun the checks, and export a packet with a clear status: “Ready for your review.” Reconnect to demonstrate the official handoff as a separate action.

**Technology:** Bundled OCR and document alignment; image-region checks on a known template; deterministic validation; an explicit dependency graph; encrypted local storage; queued sync; cached language/audio packs. AI can draft translations and extract candidate requirements for human approval. It must not invent policy or create signatures.

**72-hour scope:** One current form family, two closely related document types, English and one locally tested language, camera overlays, packet export, and source/version labels. Add one affordability scenario only if the core is stable. Use a rules snapshot for the relevant application cycle; 2026 evidence is not a claim that its deadlines remain active in October.

**Pilot / payer:** A school adviser, access NGO or bursary administrator. Learners use it free; a possible institutional fee covers assisted cases and staff time saved. This is a payer hypothesis, not a committed partnership.

**Measure:** Correctly prepared packets, preventable errors, time per correction, false warnings and false “ready” results. A blinded adviser must review samples. A tiny initial pilot tests usability, not national impact.

**Why first:** Precise evidence, emotionally understandable stakes, visible camera/voice/offline capability, and a complete user outcome without a marketplace. Main risks: stale rules, unusual family situations, unreadable images and overstating document verification.

**Pitch:** “You should not lose an opportunity because you cannot work out what a form needs. PaperBridge sees the blocker, explains it in your language, and helps you finish the next step—even offline.”

### 2 — ProofShift: carry evidence of what you can do

**Problem statement:** A young person can have practical ability from informal work while an employer sees an empty CV. How can both sides inspect credible, job-relevant evidence cheaply?

**The product:** An employer defines a short task and transparent rubric. The applicant completes a simulated stocktake, order check or till reconciliation using real objects. The phone records structured evidence and a spoken explanation. A named human reviewer signs the result, which becomes a worker-controlled evidence card.

**Demo:** Give a candidate six products and a deliberately inconsistent delivery note. Scan labels, reconcile the count and identify the mismatch. A reviewer accepts the result. A second phone scans a compact signed QR card offline. Edit the signed assessment and show verification fail. That establishes integrity and issuer identity; it does not prove that every assessment is truthful or accredited.

**Technology:** Barcode/OCR capture, supported-language STT, deterministic rubric calculations, optional CV annotations, digital signatures and cached issuer keys. A short randomized task can discourage replay. The reviewer handles competence claims; the AI does not infer employability from faces, accents or demeanour.

**MVP:** One retail task, one rubric, one reviewer, offline capture and a small signed summary. Rich media syncs later. Display the time of the last issuer/status check because offline verification cannot guarantee current revocation information.

**Pilot / payer:** Three local employers and an employment programme; employers or programmes pay for useful assessments. Paid productive trials require real employer commitments. Synthetic demonstrations must be labelled as such.

**Measure:** Blinded employer shortlisting decisions, agreement between reviewers, callbacks, paid trials, net earnings and retention. Signed cards alone are not impact.

**Why second:** Strong economic relevance and research support for credible information. It needs trust, rubric quality and actual demand. Existing assessment platforms mean portability and inspectable artifacts must earn their place.

**Pitch:** “Your first proof of ability should not require your first formal job.”

### 3 — ScamPause: check what changed before you pay

**Problem statement:** A familiar-looking supplier invoice or urgent payment request can contain changed bank details. How can a person catch the change and confirm it independently before paying?

**The product:** Share a screenshot or invoice into the app. It extracts the payee, amount, account and contact details; compares them with previously confirmed details; and highlights changes. It offers a callback to a previously trusted contact, with a short script in the user's language.

**Demo:** Present two almost identical invoices. The second has a replaced account number and telephone number. The app marks the changed digits, explains them aloud and points to the previously confirmed contact. An invoice with no suspicious change stays “not independently verified”; it does not get a universal “safe” badge.

**Technology:** Offline OCR, exact field comparisons, local trusted-contact history, URL/domain parsing, and online reputation checks when available. A bounded language model can explain signals but cannot certify a beneficiary. Initial contact verification matters as much as later comparison.

**MVP:** Three invoice layouts, one share/import flow, historical comparison, local read-aloud and a user-confirmed callback. No bank integration needed to demonstrate detection; it is not a payment-blocking feature inside another app.

**Pilot / payer:** Small-business finance staff, community organisations, banks or insurers as possible distributors. Avoid assuming sponsor API access.

**Measure:** Detection of seeded changes, false alarms on legitimate changes and whether people complete an independent check. National SABRIC losses are context, not the addressable market for this narrow feature.

**Why third:** Immediate relatability and a clear before/after demo. Trustworthy onboarding and user adoption are hard. The novelty is the complete checking workflow rather than a generic scam score.

### 4 — MarginMesh: share a replenishment plan that is worth the trip

**Problem statement:** Small traders may need only part of a wholesale case and cannot tie up tomorrow's operating cash to obtain a lower unit price. Can a small group replenish jointly while each participant saves after all costs?

**The product:** Capture a receipt and current stock; confirm prices and quantities; state tomorrow's cash commitments. A constraint solver compares buying alone with splitting cases across nearby consenting traders. It includes delivery, fees, minimum quantities, storage and the effect on each trader's cash.

**Demo:** Three sellers need different portions of a case. Show each person's stand-alone cost, share and total landed cost. Change one seller's cash limit; the plan recomputes, or explicitly says no beneficial plan exists. Generate a QR collection manifest that works locally at the collection point.

**Technology:** OCR/barcodes, fast cash entry, integer optimization, a shared order ledger, and signed handoff receipts. Forecasting starts with simple transparent estimates; ML comes after enough real sales history exists. No need to invent credit scores from noisy data.

**MVP:** Three stores, five products, two supplied price lists and one collection point. Group orders are proposals until all participants and the supplier confirm. Offline capture must not masquerade as confirmed inventory or settled payment.

**Pilot / payer:** A merchant association or wholesaler. A potential transparent fulfilment fee is acceptable only if net savings remain. Use actual price lists and observed buying patterns to test this.

**Measure:** Rand saved after all costs, cash retained, fulfilment reliability, and time spent entering data. National cash-flow statistics do not establish demand for split cases.

**Why fourth:** Strong hybrid of small business and financial inclusion. Vuleka already offers bulk-buying access, so case splitting and a transparent, individually beneficial plan are the hypothesis to validate.

### 5 — WaterWindow: make a water collection trip count

**Problem statement:** During an interruption, a household can travel to a point that is closed, depleted or overwhelmed. Can operators communicate current capacity and coordinate collection more reliably?

**The product:** A participating water point publishes confirmed stock, operating windows and accessibility. Households express need. A simple allocator proposes collection windows subject to supply and fair distribution. When the operator confirms a change, the app reallocates and clearly marks whose information is stale.

**Demo:** Reduce a simulated tank's supply and close a collection point. Show which previously feasible bookings no longer fit, an alternative plan, and the messages awaiting delivery. At one collection point, scan a signed voucher into a single local ledger to prevent duplicate redemption there.

**Technology:** Capacity scheduling, timestamps/confidence, optional calibrated level sensing, cached maps, local QR and delayed sync. Multiple disconnected redemption points cannot reliably prevent global double spending without preallocated capacity or reconciliation.

**MVP:** Two cooperating points in one ward, operator-confirmed data and one offline redemption station. With no operator, label the entire scenario as simulated.

**Pilot / payer:** A water-service operator, municipality, NGO or resilience sponsor. The app cannot guarantee water arrival, potability or receipt of an offline emergency message.

**Measure:** Failed collection trips, waiting time, data freshness and delivered volume. Compare with the actual coordination process.

**Why fifth:** Strong local relevance and visible optimization. Real supply and operating relationships dominate usefulness.

### 6 — ColdLoop: move the stock before the cold runs out

**Problem statement:** During a refrigeration failure, a small trader may lose perishable inventory while another nearby business has usable cold capacity. Can the stock be moved within a validated handling window at a cost that makes sense?

**The product:** Cooperating shops declare storage capacity and acceptance rules. Sensors or operator readings flag an excursion. The app proposes a transfer using availability, travel time, compatible storage and cost, then records custody and temperature observations.

**Demo:** Warm a sensor in your hand. A participating unit changes state. The plan moves a labelled demo crate to another unit with capacity and creates a handoff record. The alternative destination becoming unavailable triggers a revised plan. Use simulated food and validated thresholds for the demonstration.

**Technology:** Bluetooth temperature sensor, time-series anomaly alerts, constrained route/capacity scheduling, barcode identity and a local handoff ledger. ML might later estimate equipment failure from real histories; a 72-hour prototype should use explainable rules.

**MVP:** Two boxes, two sensors or clearly labelled sensor replay, one commodity, and manual operator acceptance. A camera identifies a label; it does not determine microbiological safety. A temperature alert is a handling exception, not a certificate of edibility.

**Pilot / payer:** Cold-storage operators, trader groups or insurers; potential storage or resilience subscription. Requires food-handling expertise before a live food pilot.

**Measure:** Validated handling compliance, kg and value of stock preserved, transfer cost, and operator response. The national food-loss estimate cannot be treated as recoverable inventory for this app.

**Why sixth:** Strongest physical demonstration and ambitious systems idea, but weakest build/adoption assumptions without partners. [CSIR's 2021 estimate](https://www.csir.co.za/media-hub/news/one-third-of-edible-food-in-sa-never-reaches-the-table-g20-regional-workshop-reveals) of 10.3 million tonnes of edible food lost/wasted annually is context; cold-capacity mismatch needs local evidence. ColdHubs establishes that cooling-as-a-service already exists.

### 7 — PayRhythm: plan around when money actually arrives

**Problem statement:** A monthly budget can be misleading when income comes from intermittent jobs and expenses fall due before payment arrives. Can a household see its next cash shortfall and compare practical responses?

**The product:** A person records expected and received income with confidence and timing, plus essentials and commitments. The app displays a rolling cash runway and a few clearly labelled scenarios: late payment, a smaller order, or a changed due date. It can prepare a user-reviewed payment reminder, but sends nothing automatically.

**Demo:** Move an expected payment three days later. The timeline shows the first uncovered commitment and the effect of two user-selected options. Explain the result aloud. Uncertain income remains visibly uncertain.

**Technology:** Cash ledger, recurrence rules, scenario calculation, receipt capture and language packs. Use ranges and conservative assumptions; do not pretend to predict income accurately from a few entries.

**MVP:** Manual/voice entry, one cash timeline and three scenarios, fully usable offline. No live banking dependency.

**Pilot / payer:** Informal worker organisations or enterprise support programmes. Measure forecasting error, maintained use and avoidable missed commitments. [FinScope Consumer 2025](https://www.finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/articles/media-release-finscope-consumer-south-africa-2025?entity=blog) reports nearly 9.5 million people earning informal income, using a different concept from QLFS informal-sector employment.

**Why seventh:** Relatable and buildable but a crowded personal-finance category with a less distinctive demonstration. Its best role may be a focused feature within MarginMesh.

### 8 — CareRelay: a referral that someone actually receives

**Problem statement:** A survivor seeking help may be redirected among organisations and repeatedly asked to recount sensitive events. Can a trusted worker make a consent-controlled handoff that the receiving service acknowledges?

**The product:** At a trusted service point, a worker creates a minimal, user-reviewed support summary. The survivor chooses which fields to share, which provider may receive them, and an expiry. The recipient acknowledges the referral or declines with an alternative pathway. There is no public survivor map.

**Demo:** Use fictional data to move a referral from a support worker to a shelter coordinator. Show selective field sharing, provider acceptance and withdrawal of future access. Explain that withdrawal cannot erase information already seen or independently copied.

**Technology:** Role-based access, encrypted storage, expiring links, consent receipts and source-maintained service directories. Translation assists a human worker; recording or speech is optional and situational. A safer initial product may run on the worker's managed device rather than a survivor's monitored phone.

**MVP:** One partner-defined pathway and fictional cases. Build with a specialist service; avoid autonomous risk scores, automated reports to police or claims of guaranteed dispatch.

**Pilot / payer:** NGOs and support-service networks. Measure accepted referrals, completion and repeat retelling burden with appropriate specialist review. Safety design and organisational capacity are part of the product.

**Why eighth:** The human need is profound. Ranking reflects our ability to validate and deploy responsibly in a short hackathon, not the importance of GBV. An established NGO partner could substantially improve its position.

## 6. Technology that earns its place

| Technology | Useful job | Scope discipline |
|---|---|---|
| Computer vision / OCR | Locate fields, identify document type, read printed labels and detect poor images | Support a known template and permit correction; do not equate extraction with authenticity |
| Rules / constraint solver | Apply requirements, reconcile arithmetic, allocate capacity, compare feasible plans | Every constraint has an owner and source; impossible plans remain impossible |
| STT and TTS | Let users speak, hear explanations and confirm a structured result | Test the actual language, code switching, noise and phone; offline support is a separate test |
| Local storage and queued sync | Preserve a working task when data disappears | Show pending/synced/stale states; never label an unsent request as received |
| Digital signatures / QR | Make an issued record's author and subsequent modification checkable | Signatures do not prove truth; protect private data and expose status freshness |
| Multi-agent workflow | Separate extraction, rule checks, feasibility and explanation, then flag disagreement for review | Shared schemas and evidence IDs; extra agents cannot make unsupported facts true |
| Small ML models | Improve a narrow repeated classification after collecting labelled examples | Compare against a simple baseline; do not spend the event training a foundation model |

[Google's ML Kit documentation](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/vision/text-recognition/v2/android) supports bundled on-device text recognition. [Android's offline architecture guide](https://developer.android.com/topic/architecture/data-layer/offline-first) covers local data and persistent queues. [OR-Tools](https://developers.google.com/optimization/introduction) provides scheduling and routing solvers. These make the proposed prototypes technically plausible; they do not establish field performance.

[Vulavula](https://docs.lelapa.ai/overview/introduction) offers African-language speech transcription and translation through an API. That is an online dependency. For an offline demo, use tested local speech capability or reviewed cached prompts plus typed/tapped input; label recorded audio honestly. [InkubaLM's model card](https://huggingface.co/lelapa/InkubaLM-0.4B/blob/main/README.md) has a noncommercial licence and limitations; model size alone is not a reason to build a commercial product around it.

[W3C Verifiable Credentials](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model/) explicitly separates checkable authorship from the truth of the underlying claims. A small signed receipt is enough to explore ProofShift; full standards conformance requires implementing and testing the relevant format and proof mechanisms.

For local device exchange, [Nearby Connections](https://developers.google.com/nearby/connections/overview) supports offline peer-to-peer transfer. Start with QR because it is easier to demonstrate. Neither local radio nor a cached page gives a phone connectivity to a distant server.

**UI direction:** One primary next action. Show the original evidence beside its interpretation. Use plain-language states, optional audio, large touch targets, a clear offline label and an undo/correction path. Test with shared phones and limited literacy. A polished result should let a judge understand the problem and complete the central action without your narration.

## 7. PaperBridge: the strongest build and pitch plan

The ambitious long-term idea is a reusable engine for turning complex evidence requirements into achievable steps across services. The hackathon proof is one learner completing one blocked education workflow. Expansion to procurement or other benefits follows only after that works.

### Architecture

Capture → local OCR → candidate fields with confidence → user confirmation → versioned rules → next action → reviewed packet → user-authorised official handoff.

Optional agents have four bounded jobs: extract draft fields; retrieve approved requirements; check contradictions and missing evidence; explain the result. Each output points to an evidence item. Dates, arithmetic and eligibility conditions use code and reviewed rules. One validation layer controls the final status. A confident explanation must never override a failed requirement.

### 72-hour allocation

| Window | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| Hours 0–6 | Select one supported workflow, identify official rules, define a small synthetic evaluation set, and get adviser feedback where available |
| Hours 6–24 | Capture, image quality checks, local OCR, correction UI and encrypted local records |
| Hours 24–40 | Deterministic validation, evidence/source display, language pack and clear pending/ready states |
| Hours 40–52 | Packet export, reconnection behaviour, exception cases and basic privacy controls |
| Hours 52–62 | Usability sessions, adviser review and targeted fixes |
| Hours 62–72 | Low-end-phone/airplane-mode rehearsal, accessibility polish, pitch and honest demo backup |

These are planning estimates. Preparation and prebuilt assets must follow the event's actual rules once supplied. A failed model or unavailable API should reduce scope, not silently replace a live claim with a recording.

### The demonstration in 90 seconds

1. Introduce a fictional learner with a funding opportunity and a document problem.
2. Show airplane mode on the phone and scan the paper.
3. Reveal exact highlighted omissions and let the learner hear the explanation.
4. Show a corrected sample resolving the blocker while an ambiguous field still requires human review.
5. Export the checked packet and show the source/date attached to each requirement.
6. Finish on the measurable outcome: fewer preventable mistakes and less time obtaining help. Only quote measured pilot results once collected.

### Proposed validation before committing to the pitch

These are suggested decision gates, not completed tests:

- Interview roughly 10 learners and 2 advisers about their most recent real application difficulty, without prompting them to praise our concept.
- Use at least 20 varied synthetic/sample documents with adviser-labelled outcomes. Include blur, missing pages, outdated forms, incomplete fields and valid exceptions.
- Run a counterbalanced form task with and without assistance; report the number tested and actual error counts. A small convenience sample establishes early usability only.
- Pay particular attention to false “ready” outcomes. If the system cannot distinguish a field it checked from a field it could not check, fix that before adding features.
- Seek a specific pilot commitment from a school, NGO or administrator. Public product descriptions do not substitute for demand.

If learners principally lack funding rather than paperwork help, narrow the promise and reassess. If advisers find the workflow merely duplicates an existing reliable process, switch toward ProofShift only with employer evidence, or choose ScamPause for a smaller demonstrable intervention.

## 8. What to reject

- An all-purpose youth super-app spanning jobs, funding, safety, banking and food. It spreads the user story and validation too thinly.
- A generic assistant whose output can be reproduced by pasting the same question into a chat product.
- “AI verifies skills,” “AI confirms a payment,” or “camera proves water/food is safe” without the missing verification mechanism.
- A simulated payment, government integration, dispatch or partner relationship presented as live.
- All-language offline claims without running them on the actual phone.
- Claims that a national problem total equals our market or the impact of our prototype.

The most defensible pitch connects one person's real difficulty to a tested action: what was blocked, what the app changed, what was verified, and what happened next.
