| Finding | Quote | Source | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inoculation training against imposter scams works, RCT, nationally representative US sample | [L] "the authors randomly assign participants to one of four training programs: from general tips about scams to a targeted experiential learning program inspired by inoculation theory." [L] "There is strong evidence that the inoculation process successfully and significantly increases fraud-detection without decreasing trust in real communications, and that the effects persist over time." | Robb & Wendel, UW-Madison RDRC research brief WI21-03, Sept 2021: https://rdrc.wisc.edu/files/research-briefs/WI21-03_Robb-Wendel_Research-Brief_9.27.21-(1).pdf | US, Social Security imposter scams, email. Brief gives no effect size. A search snippet claimed the effect "decays over time"; the brief says the opposite ("persist"). Use the brief. Sample "over 4,000" is from a search snippet [S], not the brief. |
| Dose is tiny | [L] "a low-cost, four-and-a-half minute training can help individuals fight fraud messaging" | same | — |
| Generalises across scam brands, NOT across channels | [L] "impact of the training generalized to non-Social Security Administration fraud, including Amazon.com imposter scams. However, the inoculation training is effective only for the type of communication addressed in the training (email) and may not apply well to other areas, as such SMSes and letters, without additional training." [L] "training should be specific to the form of communications (e.g., phone, email, letter)." | same | Design implication (inference): drills must arrive in the same channel the real scam uses (SMS, WhatsApp text, WhatsApp voice note, phone call). |
| Prebunking at social-media scale | [S] "seven preregistered studies—six randomized controlled studies (n = 6,464) and an ecologically valid field study on YouTube (n = 22,632)"; videos "improved manipulation technique recognition" | Roozenbeek, van der Linden et al., Science Advances 2022; https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/inoculateexperiment ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/pmid/36001675 | Misinformation, not financial scams. Snippet-level; verify before slides. |
| Google in-call scam protection (precedent for any "circuit breaker" idea) | [S] Android 16 blocks sideloading and accessibility-permission grants during calls with non-contacts; UK pilot: "When users on devices running Android 11 or later open a partner bank's app while sharing a screen with an unknown number, the device will show a warning screen"; partners Monzo, NatWest, Revolut | https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/13/google-announces-new-security-features-for-android-for-protection-against-scam-and-theft ; https://security.googleblog.com/2025/05/whats-new-in-android-security-privacy-2025.html | UK partner banks only as announced (May 2025). SA status NOT FOUND. A real-time in-call warning is therefore not globally novel; an SA-language version on older Android devices may still be a gap. |
