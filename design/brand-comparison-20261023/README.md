## Visual assessment — design judgment, not user-test results

| Context | C / Editorial | C + A / Spatial | Recommended use |

| Formal letter | Particularly strong: name-first authority, fewer graphic elements. | Equally formal when symbol is modest; large emblem is unnecessary. | Wordmark alone or small refined lockup; do not turn letterhead into a poster. |
| Presentation | Strong editorial opening and elegant typography. | Spatial mark and contour motif connect more directly to interactive environments. | Refined opening/closing; restrained C-led content slides, small footer symbol. |

## Recommendation

Keep C as the primary visual personality and typographic system. Use A's refined symbol as its compact signature rather than treating the two as competing brands. A single identity can have a wordmark-only treatment for formal text and a symbol for icons/avatars. Avoid switching arbitrarily between a serif K icon and the spatial icon across product touchpoints: that would weaken recognition. The serif K remains a comparison alternative, not an additional adopted app mark.

This is an art-direction recommendation, not evidence of measured recognition or trademark exclusivity. Before final adoption, check icon recognition at actual sizes, contrast, accessible text, name legibility, email rendering, LinkedIn cropping, print and screen colour; test with people who have not seen the design brief.
