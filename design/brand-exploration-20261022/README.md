# KAIRO — image-first identity exploration

22 October 2026. Three distinct concept boards generated with the built-in image_gen tool, using the imagegen and brandkit skills. The user's classroom/teacher references inform warm daylight, ivory, olive clothing, deep green and a confident human presence. They were visual references in the conversation, not image-edit targets. The rejected initial book/speech/shield identity is not adopted or overwritten.

## Art-director recommendation

A is the strongest starting point for a distinct spatial product mark. C best captures the warm, mature editorial tone of the reference. B is a useful challenger for a more energetic product identity. Do not indiscriminately combine all three: first choose the brand's personality, then refine one coherent mark and typography system. None is asserted to be unique in the trademark sense.

## Application rules to prove in the next design round

- Ivory/white shirt: dark forest or ink embroidery. Olive/dark shirt: cream/light single-colour embroidery. Avoid tone-on-tone where the primary badge must be recognized quickly.
- Charcoal shirt/background: light inverse mark; brass/ochre is optional detail, not the only readable element. No thin gold outlines carrying the identity.
- Documents: a clean single-ink logo that survives ordinary office printers and black-and-white copies. Keep foil/deboss as optional finishes, not essential recognition.
- Cards/posters: show both flat colour proofs and physical mockups; a beautiful shadow or embossed finish cannot rescue weak geometry.
- App: 16/24/32px favicon/icon checks, 48px launcher tile, light/dark header and readable adjacent wordmark. No dependence on tiny subtext.
- Teacher shirt: small left-chest mark, high contrast, tested with UVs, mipmaps, folds, lighting and distance. Do not paint the approved texture onto the current rig before a character task claims the files.

## Required refinement before adoption

Redraw the selected logo in clean vectors; optically correct curves/corners and kerning; remove generated inconsistencies; create primary, compact, icon, inverse and one-ink masters. Set minimum sizes and clear space from actual tests. Verify textile/print reproduction and license the selected production fonts or draw original lettering. Conduct originality/trademark screening. Test legibility and recognition with users. A concept board is not evidence of legal uniqueness, production font ownership or final print/device acceptance.

The blueprint is separately open to Claude's independent technical review. Its document length or the appearance of this branding is not proof the engineering design is correct. The architecture, cost assumptions, source claims and acceptance gates must stand on their own evidence.
