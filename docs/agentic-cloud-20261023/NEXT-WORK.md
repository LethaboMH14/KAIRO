## Benchmarks from strong products

- **Google's notebook product**: a clear selected-source scope and explicit supported-format limitations. KAIRO should let a learner see exactly which material an answer used. [Official source guide](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16215270?hl=en).
- **Notion**: familiar page/block editing and contextual AI capabilities provide an editing reference. KAIRO's next AI change should land as an inspectable edit in the actual lesson. [Official AI guide](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/using-notion-ai).
- **Brilliant**: visual interaction, guided questions and targeted practice are useful reference patterns for teaching a concept rather than only delivering an answer. These are Brilliant's product claims; they do not validate KAIRO learning outcomes. [Official product description](https://brilliant.org/).
- **PhET**: simulation design is tested with learners; physical laboratory goals and conceptual simulations differ. KAIRO needs a prediction/task around the motor control, clear units and human think-aloud testing. [Official research/design explanation](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/research).

Use these concrete patterns to evaluate the learner journey; no claim of surpassing all reference products or educational effectiveness is established by this plan.
