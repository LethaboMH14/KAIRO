# SASL-RIGHTS-001 — data, model and permission evidence

**Owner:** Claude (`claude-classroom-20261023-a`, Opus 5.5, effort max). **Checked:** 23 October 2026. **Status:** research only. Nothing was downloaded for training, no video corpus was reconstructed, no camera was opened and no model was trained.

This extends [SASL-BIDIRECTIONAL.md](../../experience/SASL-BIDIRECTIONAL.md). Every licence claim below was checked on the original page, or else marked unverified. Quotes are short and literal.

## Bottom line

1. **No SASL source found today gives permitted, consented and annotated video for training a recogniser in a commercial education product.** The most relevant corpus (UCT 2024, 5,047 sentence pairs) has no stated licence or availability. The open-licensed SASL items are single videos or figures with no annotation. The two annotated or collected academic sets are non-commercial, or are not video at all.
2. **The landmark models that work in the browser (MediaPipe hand, pose and face) are Apache-2.0** according to their model cards. They are feature extractors for AR and gesture use. Their cards place occlusion, multiple people, a hidden head, and identity or surveillance uses out of scope. Signing involves hand-over-face contact and fast motion. These models are therefore suitable for a **tracking-only** pilot that is measured with signers, never for a translation claim.
3. **Recognition data must be created for KAIRO with Deaf co-designers under explicit, separated consent.** Such data covers a bounded intent domain, multiple signers and a signer-disjoint evaluation. Signed output starts as **human-signed, reviewed clips** under a manifest. Nothing in this pass changes the existing honest "not ready" states in `/classroom`.

## Legal and consent context

- **SASL is an official language.** Parliament reported that the National Assembly approved the Constitution Eighteenth Amendment Bill on 2 May 2023 ([press release, 3 May 2023](https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/na-approves-south-african-sign-language-12th-official-language)), to "ensure the realisation of the rights of persons who are deaf and hard of hearing". Reported assent was 19 July 2023, in Gazette 49041. Commencement by proclamation is **not verified here**.
- **POPIA:** the Information Regulator's [guidance note](https://inforegulator.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/InfoRegSA-GuidanceNote-Processing-SpecialPersonalInformation-20210628.pdf) (28 June 2021) says section 26 "prohibits the processing of special personal information", which lists "biometric information", subject to section 27(1). One exception is processing "carried out with the consent of a data subject". The statutory definition of *biometrics* was not retrieved in this pass. The design therefore assumes landmark sequences of face, hands and body may count as biometric information. It keeps them on the device, unstored, and covered by a specific consent. Legal review is still required.
- **Consent is three separate things:** consent to communicate (live, on-device), to store (recordings or landmarks), and to train or publish. A CC licence on a video does not replace a signer's consent to ML use of their likeness.

## What would unblock recognition (SASL-INTENT-001)

1. Deaf co-designers choose a bounded adult-learning intent set, such as "repeat", "slower", "explain this number", "I don't understand" and "next step", together with its regional variants. Nobody invents signs from English labels.
2. Recording happens under a written consent covering each use separately, with at least 10 signers (the target is to be agreed with the reviewers), varied devices, lighting, handedness and skin tones, and out-of-domain negatives.
3. Evaluation is signer-disjoint, with per-intent recall, false-accept and abstention rates, and a per-signer and per-device breakdown. Release thresholds are agreed with the reviewers before the pilot.
4. Signed output (SASL-OUTPUT-001) means human-signed clips per lesson intent, reviewed by fluent Deaf reviewers and recorded in a manifest (see [CAPTURE-DESIGN.md](CAPTURE-DESIGN.md) → contracts).

## Limits of this pass

Search coverage is not exhaustive: university repositories, DeafSA, PanSALB and SADiLaR items were not all inspected. Real SASL, the AfriSign data terms and the POPIA *biometrics* definition remain unverified. No legal advice is given. No Deaf co-designer or reviewer has been consulted, and that cannot be simulated.
