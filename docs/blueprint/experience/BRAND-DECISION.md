# Confirmed identity: rounded pathmark

**Decision owner:** user. **Confirmed:** 23 October 2026. **Status:** direction selected; final vector/reproduction acceptance pending.

## Identify it by appearance

The selected identity is the rounded continuous-path K-like symbol with a detached upper-right rounded stroke, geometric uppercase **KAIRO** wordmark and broad flowing line patterns. The user calls this **C**. Use [this exact reference](../../../design/app-experience-20261023/assets/brand-reference.png) to resolve any older filename/letter confusion. Do not substitute the sharp two-plane architectural mark or serif wordmark. That alternative remains reserved for a future use, with no present assignment.

The path can express continuity from understanding to practical evidence to a next step. This is our intended brand story, not a claim about the historical etymology or legal uniqueness of the symbol. The letters and shape should stand on their own without an explanatory tagline.

| Token | Value | Intended use |

| Chalk | `#F5F0E6` | Main surfaces, reverse logo on moss |
| Moss | `#3E5144` | Primary mark, text emphasis, primary actions, navigation |

Use Graphite/Moss for readable small text on Chalk; do not assume Sage/Copper pass text contrast. Never encode success, warnings or progress only by colour. Verify all actual foreground/background pairs before release.

## System across the app and materials

- **App:** compact mark + KAIRO in the header; mark-only app icon when space is limited. No new competing mascot symbol. The teacher is a person-like learning companion, not the logo.
- **Rain’s shirt:** small flat one-colour chest badge. Cream on dark moss; moss on cream/sage if contrast holds. Avoid metallic gradients or an embossed effect in the actual texture. Respect Rain asset attribution and change provenance.
- **Documents, CV exports, emails and letters:** modest flat logo, generous margins, conventional readable body typography. Do not make generated learner documents look like an accredited institutional certificate.
- **Cards, notebooks and posters:** broad path pattern can crop at the edge. Keep it behind the main reading area; a pattern is not a second logo.
- **Presentations and LinkedIn:** keep full lockup in safe areas, large quiet space, one focal statement; test avatar and banner crops separately. No social publication is authorised by this design task.
- **3D treatments:** embossing, paper relief and soft light are optional presentation treatments. The flat master must work first on a plain light or dark background.

## Typography and layout

Use locally bundled **Manrope** for the interface (SIL OFL supplied with font). The chosen geometric logo lettering remains its own artwork; ordinary Manrope text in the prototype is not the finished wordmark. Do not return to serif display headings inside the selected system without a deliberate new review.

Spacing: 4/8/12/16/24/32/48 px scale; main desktop content max 1,510 px including gutters; readable prose max roughly 70 characters. At narrow widths, retain the main action, use one content column, and put teaching responses below the room rather than covering the board. Text labels accompany icons.

## Required final-master package

1. Precisely construct symbol and wordmark as editable paths; compare overlays against the selected reference. Record any intentional adjustments.
2. Produce full lockup, horizontal lockup, symbol, monochrome and reversed SVG/PDF assets with viewBox, clear space and approved minimum sizes. No embedded font dependency for the master wordmark.
3. Test 16/24/32 px icons, small shirt embroidery, one-colour stationery, photocopy, dark/cream/sage fabrics, presentation projection and phone crops. Simplify only in a documented micro-mark if needed.
4. Check rounded corners, stroke gaps, optical alignment, raster exports and transparent backgrounds. An app icon may have a background tile; the logo master does not require one.
5. Keep provenance and perform appropriate similarity/trademark review before external brand rollout. Direction selection alone is not legal clearance.

The alternative is not deleted or renamed. No selected reference image has been overwritten.
