# KAIRO Access: learning product blueprint

**Status:** Proposed product and research blueprint, 22 October 2026. It is not an implementation specification, accredited curriculum, validated intervention, employer commitment, or learning-outcome claim. Pilot audience: adults aged 18+ at university and TVET, per the user's direction. The six studios and shared teacher remain the wider product vision.

## Product promise and boundaries

KAIRO Access should help a learner turn a difficult source or practical question into understanding they can explain, use in a new situation, and show as inspectable evidence. The repeatable loop is: choose a goal → bring or find a source → inspect and ground it → learn with a suitable representation → retrieve and practise → try a changed problem → reflect and save evidence → choose a next step.

The distinctive mechanism is an **anchored teaching conversation**. A learner points to a sentence, diagram, board mark, model part, video timestamp, or task decision and asks “why?”, “show me”, “rewind that step”, or “test me somewhere else”. KAIRO resolves the anchor to an approved source or authored scene target, gives a cited explanation or deterministic action, and lets the learner retry. If target resolution or source grounding fails, KAIRO says so and offers the readable excerpt or a safe fallback.

The local foundation already has reviewed source extraction, cited lessons, a Lecture Theatre, contextual passage controls, browser-local progress, an authored stock-control case, role-preparation material, and an Engineering Studio. These are development features, not evidence of learning efficacy, multi-user privacy, credentials, employer endorsement, or production readiness. Rain is a separately reviewed CC-BY asset but is not integrated in /teacher. Current voice rehearsal does not prove natural lip sync, multilingual coverage, or SASL capability.

No guaranteed learning gain, job, placement, accredited credit, qualification, SME revenue increase, or employer partnership is claimed. FNB/employer incentives are hypotheses only, requiring direct approval, transparent terms, learner choice and privacy review. A completion badge must never silently become a credential.

## Adult university/TVET onboarding

Keep onboarding short, skippable and useful without a CV. A learner can inspect a sample before creating a profile or adding personal details.

1. **Access and language:** Set interface, teaching, source, answer and assessment language separately, plus captions/transcript, text scale, contrast, motion and optional voice/3D. Learner chooses; do not infer from name or location.
2. **Goal:** Understand a course concept, prepare for assessment, practise a work task, build a portfolio artifact, explore a field, or “not sure”. Do not diagnose motivation or employability.
3. **Course discovery:** Search a dated catalogue by institution, course/module code, topic, level, language and material type. Distinguish exact from related matches; show prerequisites, source, review date and coverage gaps. Do not imply institutional alignment without evidence.
4. **Source:** Start from a sample, reviewed public resource, user link/reference or supported upload. Inspect OCR before teaching; correct or reject it. Preserve source revision and citations.
5. **Small diagnostic:** One or two low-stakes questions plus optional confidence and “not seen this”. Diagnose response patterns (vocabulary, ambiguity, prerequisite, calculation), never the person.
6. **Teaching choice:** Read, listen, captions, optional teacher/3D or low-data mode. Interrupt, rewind, ask another explanation, change language or stop narration without losing place.
7. **Practice:** Worked example → partial step → independent changed case. Label help used; accessibility support is not misconduct.
8. **Evidence and next step:** Show attempts, source support, hints, retries, delayed independent result and uncertainty. Distinguish self-described, practised, independently reassessed, externally reviewed, credentialed and host-confirmed.
9. **Optional profile/CV:** Name, bio, image, education, projects, work history, CV import and opportunity preferences are opt-in. Preview every extracted field and source span; require approval. Never invent dates, grades, proficiency, outcomes, references or protected traits.
10. **Account/continuity:** Authentication, OTP, recovery, data location, retention, export and deletion require a separate reviewed privacy/security design. No claim that account flows are implemented.

## Spoken multilingual and SASL contract

“Two-way multilingual” separates **input language, teaching language, source language, answer language, caption language, assessment language and voice**. A learner can ask in one supported language and receive an answer in another while original evidence remains visible. This does not mean all directions, dialects, voices, subjects or SASL are available.

Prioritize evaluated Setswana and isiZulu paths selected by the learner. Record language, direction, code-switching and technical terms in evaluation. Review each independently with fluent educators/community reviewers; preserve source quotations; maintain a glossary; label uncertain translation; compare outcomes across language settings. Government policy groups isiZulu with Nguni and Setswana with Sotho languages. That grouping does not make the languages interchangeable.

SASL is a separate pathway with Deaf SASL educators, interpreters, linguists and learners. Spoken ASR/TTS and captions are not SASL. Generic sign video is not a SASL translation. Signed content needs review of handshape, movement, location, orientation, timing, facial grammar, eye gaze, posture, spatial references and framing. Begin with licensed human-recorded lessons in a bounded topic set if rights/reviewer capacity allow. No generated avatar signing is validated here.

Any model/API candidate needs an audit of weights/data licence, source rights, intended use, evaluation representation, dialect/code-switch coverage, quality per direction, privacy, safety, hosting and cost. A model card or Hugging Face listing is a lead, not KAIRO capability. No private CV/coursework fine-tuning by default.

## Pedagogical rationale and assessment

IES What Works Clearinghouse recommends spaced review, interleaved worked examples/problems, graphics plus verbal descriptions, abstract/concrete representations, retrieval quizzes, metacognitive judgments and explanatory questions; evidence ratings differ by recommendation. This is school-oriented guidance, not a KAIRO effect-size promise. EEF feedback guidance says effective feedback builds on strong initial instruction/formative assessment and should be actionable; poorly designed feedback can fail. CAST UDL 3.0 (released 30 July 2024) promotes multiple means of engagement, representation and action/expression. These sources support choice and alternatives; they do not prove a 3D teacher improves learning.

Operationally: state the goal; show a short worked example with evidence anchor; ask learner to retrieve/explain a step; give a small actionable hint; fade support and change context; revisit later with a new item; compare confidence with observed performance without shame. Keep teacher interruptible. Do not narrate over dense reading, add decorative motion, infer emotion, assume “learning styles”, reward only speed, or let a generic model decide regulated professional answers.

Record per **objective × task family × language × response mode × support level**, with task/rubric/source versions. Capture first answer, result, hints/replay/translation, confidence, retry, feedback and delayed reassessment. Screen time, gaze, voice tone, face, clicks and streaks are not mastery evidence.

Visible states: **not attempted**, **introduced**, **practised with support**, **independent on this item**, **independently reassessed on a changed item**, **reviewed externally**, **credential/host-confirmed**. “Mastery” is only a provisional learning recommendation after predeclared repeated performance on distinct tasks, including delayed/changed-context work. Show evidence and uncertainty; completion is not a credential.

| Evaluation question | Test | Measures/limits |
|---|---|---|
| Did the lesson teach it? | Pre-register goal; expert-review parallel baseline/post items; compare with accessible text-first lesson. | Immediate/delayed performance, item/rubric agreement; no qualification claim. |
| Does learner transfer? | Held-out scenario with changed values/context and explanation. | Independent accuracy, reasoning, evidence choice and hints; blind review where practical. |
| Does 3D help? | Same content in 2D and optional 3D. | Learning/transfer plus latency, cognitive load, access parity and opt-out. |
| Do languages preserve meaning? | Paired Setswana/isiZulu items, both input/output directions and code-switch cases, expert reviewed. | Semantic/technical fidelity, ASR/TTS where relevant, answer correctness, corrections; report each direction. |
| Is access equivalent? | Co-design with disabled, keyboard/screen-reader and Deaf SASL learners. | Objective completion, blocked controls, caption/sign quality, recovery and discomfort. |
| Is work preparation credible? | Synthetic task based on dated primary employer/professional criteria; independent artifact rubric. | Accuracy, transfer, reproducibility and reviewer agreement; no hiring prediction. |

**Synthetic workplace protocol:** show a synthetic-data banner; version stable seed, data dictionary, answer key, domain reviewer and rubric. Include damage, timing, duplicate, missing-field and legitimate discrepancy records so unsupported accusations do not score. Test novice, hint/translation users, keyboard/2D, offline recovery, ambiguous source, malicious embedded instructions and changed case. No private/customer/employer data. A simulation is not work experience.

## Bounded distinctive interactions

- **Anchor rewind:** return to an authored step, source span and scene target; unclear target asks for confirmation.
- **Show me why:** unfold citation, assumptions, deterministic calculation and counterexample; unknown remains visible.
- **Different explanation:** learner chooses example, diagram, plain/technical explanation or worked steps; not a learning-style diagnosis.
- **Same principle, new world:** change values/context within validated bounds; ask what stayed invariant.
- **Evidence ledger:** first try, helped try, retry, delayed independent result and reviewer state stay separate.
- **Evidence/interpretation split:** display source statement, lesson inference and unresolved question separately.
- **Teach-back handover:** explain decision to fictional colleague; rubric checks evidence, clarity and uncertainty. Speech optional.
- **Timed speech:** word/phrase alignment supports navigation; phoneme timing for lip motion is a separate evaluation and acceptance gate.
- **Tap-to-explain:** number opens meaning, units, source and consequence; gaze is not required.
- **“I'm stuck” interrupt:** stop audio immediately, including mid-sentence; preserve the learner's heard prefix and a checkpoint, cancel stale queued actions, then offer a small hint. Resume only after the learner chooses a semantic boundary. Touch/keyboard works without voice/gaze.
- **Listening nod:** subtle authored acknowledgement while learner types/speaks; no emotion inference and no effect on scoring.
- **Phone low-data mode:** text and compressed stills first, selective media, no autoplay, optional 3D; finish every objective without 3D.
