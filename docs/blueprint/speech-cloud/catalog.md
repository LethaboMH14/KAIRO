# Speech model and dataset catalog

**Evidence date:** 22 October 2026. Hugging Face repository metadata and model cards are not independent product-quality evaluations. Access gates, licenses and source-data terms can change; recheck the exact revision and full terms before access, deployment, or commercial use. “Commercial-compatible” below only describes the displayed model/data licence field; it does not cure upstream data rights, attribution, privacy, voice consent, or separate terms.

## How to interpret these candidates

- **STT/ASR** maps spoken audio into text.
- **TTS** maps text into generated audio. Voice synthesis has additional speaker consent and impersonation concerns.
- **Text MT** maps text between languages; it is neither STT nor TTS and is not SASL.
- **Lip sync** needs timestamps associated with the exact audio. A list of mouth shapes and timestamps is not the same as word-level alignment.
- **SASL translation/production** involves visual signing, linguistic grammar, non-manual markers, signer identity and community authorization. Speech model tags never make a model a sign-language system.

Do not train on learner microphone data or upload it to an evaluation provider. Any new native-speaker recording plan requires an approved purpose, consent form, compensation, retention/deletion policy, whether voice cloning/synthesis is allowed or explicitly prohibited, withdrawal terms, a separated locked test set, and a community-governed access arrangement. A dataset made for ASR is not automatically authorized for TTS.

## SASL research and access status

UCT's 2024 paper *Vision-Based automatic translation for South African Sign Language* reports a research corpus with 5,047 government/politics sentences and six sign interpreters, three native signers. It is evidence that SASL research exists, not proof that the model/code/video is open, licensed for commercial use, sufficiently general, or appropriate for education. [UCT repository record](https://open.uct.ac.za/items/fdf2c98d-8f20-4629-9fbe-41421886c94b), [UCT Research Portal summary](https://www.eraonline.uct.ac.za/converis/portal/detail/Publication/608280969?lang=en_GB).

The legacy ATIS sign-language corpus index lists South African Sign Language among several corpus languages, but access and licensing are not established by that index. It must not be treated as a readily usable public KAIRO dataset without checking the original paper/source and rights. [ACL Anthology ATIS record](https://aclanthology.org/L08-1470/).

No directly usable, licence-cleared, general-purpose open SASL signing-generation model/dataset was verified in this research. This is a statement about the search and evidence available, **not a claim that none exists**. A first product slice should be a Deaf-led, authored set of human-signed, lesson-specific videos/glossary entries, explicitly labeled and captioned, with review and updating. Never fabricate sign sequences from an English/isiZulu/Tswana gloss or animate Rain's generic hands as if that were SASL.
