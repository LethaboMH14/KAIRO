# Research, business decisions and evidence gaps

24 October 2026. Public sources establish context and listed costs. They do not establish KAIRO demand or effectiveness. All links below were reviewed in this work or in the bounded Luna research memo; exact cloud SKUs and procurement terms still need an actual quote/billing export. No browser observation, account mutation or portal submission occurred.

## Who receives value, and who pays?

The first learner job: “Help me work through this difficult concept from my own course, see why it behaves that way, attempt it myself and keep my working.” A department or tutoring programme may have a different job: “Help us deliver a reviewable activity for this cohort without losing control of the material or creating unbounded support cost.” Neither job is validated by national statistics. Interview both roles; a student who likes the avatar cannot approve a university purchase.

Choose one adult engineering module with an existing motor/pump activity first. Computing/Information Systems is adjacent because the API activity exists; broad medicine, law, every engineering field and workplace recruitment are not in the initial sales promise. This narrowing reduces domain-validation, training, content-rights and onboarding costs. It also gives the learner a clear reason to enter the app. New module codes need instructor mapping; similar titles do not prove curriculum equivalence.

The core deliverable is an attempted explanation/practice task tied to a reviewed source, with saved working. Offer reading and learner control without penalising users who skip the avatar. Avoid presenting a generated answer, practice completion or gamified level as an accredited qualification or employer-certified skill.

## Market sizing without invented market share

National system context: DHET's public HEI population. Serviceable segment: adults in specific supported technical modules whose content rights, device needs and teaching goals fit the product. Its exact size is unknown. Obtain a named module roster count with permission, not scraped personal student records. Reachable pilot: 20–40 consenting learners through one willing course/support team; this is a recruitment target, not an agreement. The model's seven 250-seat contracts are an illustration of the commercial volume needed, not a measured serviceable obtainable market.

Do not add the same learner in calculus, motors and programming three times. The commercial billing unit must be a unique licensed learner for the contract period; repeated modules can add content/setup work, not fictitious extra people. Existing fictional judge profiles demonstrate tailored journeys, not university affiliation or real student consent.

## FNB and major-company methods, appropriately applied

[FNB Instant Cashflow](https://www.fnb.co.za/business-banking/instant-solutions/instant-cashflow.html) describes projecting from a business's actual account transactions and allowing changes to variables. KAIRO has no commercial transaction history. We therefore use a driver-based budget and scenarios, with explicit collection delays, instead of pretending to have trained a bank-quality forecast. When there are actual paid invoices and costs, reconcile monthly actuals to this plan and replace assumptions.

FirstRand's public 2025 financial-results reporting describes baseline and additional macroeconomic scenarios in its impairment models ([issuer publication](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-analysis-of-financial-results-June-2025-single-page-printable-booklet.pdf); [JSE copy](https://senspdf.jse.co.za/documents/2025/jse/isse/fsr/FSR0625.pdf)). Search-index excerpts were accessible; direct PDF retrieval failed. We borrow only the general discipline of testing multiple adverse conditions. This pack does not reproduce a proprietary FNB credit model, IFRS 9 validation or a bank capital test. No bank endorsement, lending eligibility or partnership is asserted.

Tools used: a reproducible Python driver model; an editable formula-based Excel workbook using the bundled spreadsheet tool; native two-variable price/voice sensitivity; deterministic scenario and reconciliation checks; seeded Monte Carlo with published uncalibrated priors; independent Luna and chat reviews. These are proportionate FP&A tools for a pre-revenue product. ML demand forecasting now would add apparent precision without observations. Later, use cohort actuals and backtesting against a simple baseline before any predictive model is trusted.

## Psychology translated into a respectful product

| Learner concern | Design response / test | Business implication |
|---|---|---|
| “I don't understand, and I feel behind.” | A small concrete objective, a correctable source and an initial low-pressure attempt; test whether wording reduces confusion. | Sell a useful task, not fear of failing or unemployment. |
| “Is the AI making this up?” | Direct source passage/citation, uncertainty and an edit/review path. Count critical errors and teacher-review minutes. | Quality assurance is a cost, not a free consequence of using a model. |
| “This is too much on my phone/data.” | Reading first, deliberate 3D loading, visible progress/cancel, saved work. Measure transferred bytes and task recovery. | Expensive animation/voice cannot be mandatory for basic value. |
| “I forgot where I stopped.” | Resume the actual task and show saved status; reminders only on opt-in. | Retention should follow usefulness, not coercive streaks. |
| “Will this get me a job?” | Show learner working and next practice, with clear unverified status. | No recruitment fee/reward revenue until real consented partner terms and verified outcomes exist. |

## Validation, growth and stop rules

1. **Discover:** interview 8–12 adults and 3–5 tutors/budget owners using recent concrete examples. Ask about current alternatives, actual spend, course constraints and who approves a purchase. Record contradictions, not just supportive quotes. This sample is not representative prevalence research.
2. **Usability:** 5–8 adults attempt reviewed-source → lesson → practice → saved resume on their actual device, with consent and separately authorised observation. Planning gate: at least 80% finish without intervention; zero unresolved critical privacy/source errors. Small samples give formative findings, not production acceptance. Existing browser restriction is not relaxed by this plan.
3. **Learning pilot:** 20–40 participants, one module and reviewer-approved objectives/questions. Assess prior knowledge, immediate and delayed transfer where possible; retain attrition and uncertainty. A controlled comparator is needed for causal efficacy claims. Budget and content/privacy approval precede recruitment.
4. **Buyer test:** present a specific deliverable, allowance, delivery dates, invoice/VAT basis, support terms and full cost to a named budget owner. At least one real paid or formally funded commitment is the gate to a commercial pilot. Interviews, letters of interest and promised introductions are not revenue.
5. **Meter:** collect minimal consented operational data (call tokens/characters, extraction duration, bytes, retries, support time). Compare measured p50/p95 cost and failure rates against the allowance; no raw source text in cost logs. Replace the provisional infra reserve and price assumptions before selling scale.
6. **Expand one approved module/cohort at a time:** measure learning task completion, repeat use and renewal separately. No paid acquisition expansion while support, security or contribution gates fail. No borrowing/funding commitment is made by the model.
7. **Stop/iterate:** if there is no clear buyer/budget, inaccurate source-to-practice mapping, unacceptable data/device burden, unaffordable price, critical safety/privacy issue or no margin at acceptable support levels, narrow the offer or stop the segment. Reforecast each month from actuals. Do not continue spending the 24-month zero-demand budget just because it is modelled.

## Commercial readiness gates and overlooked costs

Before charging: genuine learner/institution contracts; applicable privacy/retention/processor terms and international-transfer assessment; content/3D/model/font/voice licences; security incident and support process; accessibility/device acceptance; billing/cancellation/refund terms and accounting review; tenant/roster separation and unique-seat billing; tested per-account entitlements and daily/global spend containment. Current pilot quotas and IAM-private release do not establish any of these at commercial scale.

No blanket SASL translation, arbitrary simulation creation, guaranteed multilingual accuracy or clinical/workplace certification is included. Additional language/sign-language validation, specialist educators, licensed assets, live search feeds, heavy video/vision, GPU training/hosting, enterprise SSO/LMS integrations, external audits/insurance and larger staffing require separately scoped quotes. The current budget has reserves, not an assertion that every future liability is covered.
