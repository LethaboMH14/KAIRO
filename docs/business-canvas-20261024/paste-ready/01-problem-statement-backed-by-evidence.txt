Learners can have course material but still struggle to understand it, apply it, demonstrate a useful skill or see how it connects to a career. They also have to navigate role requirements, projects, certificates, bursaries/NSFAS and application deadlines across separate services. Language, disability, device/data limitations and limited practical access can add barriers. KAIRO is being built to connect personalised learning, practical evidence and relevant opportunities, initially for adult engineering and computing/Information Systems learners, then institution and company training environments. Local frequency, severity and willingness to pay still need direct research.

South Africa’s public HEIs recorded 1,115,580 enrolments in 2024 (DHET PSET 2024). Stats SA’s GHS 2024 reports 75.6% household mobile internet access but 17.4% fixed internet at home. These support an access-conscious educational focus, not an assumed paying market. Retrieval/feedback research informs our teaching approach. Our intended contribution to unemployment is to help learners build relevant, inspectable working and prepare for opportunities; learning gains, successful applications and employment effects must be measured separately rather than promised.
