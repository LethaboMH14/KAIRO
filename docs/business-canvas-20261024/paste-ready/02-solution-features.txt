KAIRO’s full journey is: learner profile/course/year/goals and optional confirmed CV → authorised multimodal material → reviewed, cited lesson curated to the person and learning objective → KAIRO teaching in a relevant studio or reading view → practice, feedback and adaptation → saved evidence/skill profile → sourced role requirements and a gap-specific learning/project/certificate plan → relevant jobs, bursaries/NSFAS, hackathons and updates → learner-controlled applications and employer sharing. Spoken-language access, listening and SASL are part of the intended journey. Companies can use approved internal material and competency requirements for role-specific workforce training.

The current foundation includes private materials, cited editable lessons, prepared motor/pump/API activities, saved working, synthetic course profiles, manual role preparation and CV drafting. Full adaptive integration, visual/audio understanding, fresh opportunity feeds, automated role alignment, employer/company workspaces and validated language/SASL services remain delivery work. Current camera tracking is not SASL translation. These are central planned capabilities, not removed from the product or claimed as already validated.
