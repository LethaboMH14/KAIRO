## How the film feels

The student is already trying. They have information, deadlines and ambition, but cannot organise the next step. Questions arrive in the same spatial language as the interface eventually uses. At the reveal, KAIRO does not blast them away: she helps organise them. That continuity is the signature visual idea.

The centre remains a person, then a comprehensible motor. Each move has a reason: paper/source becomes motor, prediction becomes observation, explanation becomes retained working. The final emotion is “I have a way through.” Employment and funding fears can supply context, but an absent live workflow cannot be shown as solved. A current role-preparation note is not a successful job application or employer endorsement.
