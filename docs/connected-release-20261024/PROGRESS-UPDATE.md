# Paste-ready progress update — 24 October 2026

## What has been accomplished?

We connected KAIRO's account and private-material foundation to the learner app: upload or paste material, review the extraction, generate a source-linked lesson, edit it, learn through reading or KAIRO, practise and save progress. Durable processing jobs can be reopened after reload. Motor and pump studios now have prepared, cited teaching steps and contextual guidance; pump practice can produce downloadable evidence. The application has been built for Google Cloud Run with Firebase Authentication, Firestore, private object storage, a controlled import queue, Vertex AI and Google voice.

The installed automated suite passed 635/635 checks, with an additional import-recovery regression and 12 focused trace checks passing. Real synthetic cloud checks used two disposable accounts to verify private-record isolation, extraction jobs, review, editable lessons, saved progress, model generation, voice and sign-out revocation. These are engineering checks, not claims of measured learning improvement or final user acceptance.

## What challenges are we facing?

Documents containing mixed scans, diagrams, formulas and charts still need better multimodal evaluation and learner review. Current extraction and generation have visible size and coverage limits. We have not yet completed mobile, screen-reader or human learner acceptance. Simulations are prepared educational activities, not automatically validated models generated from any upload. Institutional tenancy, payments, employer publishing, trained mastery models and continuous signed-language translation remain further work.

## What will we focus on next?

Verify the complete hosted journey with a real learner when device observation is permitted; improve material review for mixed documents; deepen the predict–experiment–explain–evidence loop in the motor and pump studios; validate account recovery and data-retention/deletion workflows; and record a truthful end-to-end demonstration for the pitch and submission deck. Evaluate usability and actual learning outcomes before making impact or traction claims.
