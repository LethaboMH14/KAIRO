# Learner CV workspace

Root-owned integration of the versioned `kairo-cv-draft-v1` model. Route `/cv`; no profile reads, persistence, provider calls or applications.

Design: ivory #f7f3e9 paper, olive #44583e controls, ink #263126, muted #626958, line #d9dacb. Existing local serif stack for the page title and system sans for forms. A workbench: editable details on the left, an ordinary selectable text document on the right, stacking on small screens. The document is the focal point; decoration and animation do not help this task.

Learner flow: add a detail under a chosen section; confirm its wording; review the draft; download plain text. Editing a detail revokes its confirmation. Any form change invalidates the old preview and prevents a stale download. Employer requirements stay separate from applicant facts. No facts are imported from practice, inferred, or upgraded to experience. The learner controls every category and confirmation; KAIRO has not verified their statements.

Details remain in this tab only. Leaving warns about in-memory work. Plain-text export contains confirmed details only; it does not preserve unconfirmed details or target requirements. No ATS pass prediction, DOCX/PDF, cloud sync, account memory, application submission or browser download receipt is claimed. Browser interaction/accessibility acceptance remains pending.
