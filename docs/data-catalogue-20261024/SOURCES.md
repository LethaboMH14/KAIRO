# Source register

## Rights and source material

- [OpenStax current licensing information](https://help.openstax.org/s/article/Licensing-information-of-OpenStax-textbooks) — current general guide says its textbook library uses CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and commercial reuse needs permission.
- [OpenStax University Physics Volume 2, §11.4](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/11-4-magnetic-force-on-a-current-carrying-conductor) and [chapter introduction/licensing notice](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/11-introduction) — current chapter derives magnetic force on a current-carrying conductor; the current book page states CC BY-NC-SA and prior written permission is required for generative-AI ingestion. Link-only candidate; no copied content.
- [OpenStax Calculus Volume 1 preface](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/preface) and [index/licensing notice](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/index) — current online edition states CC BY-NC-SA and prior written permission for LLM training/ingestion into generative-AI offerings. An older 2021 PDF has been reported as CC BY, but this pass did not verify an exact official file/version licence; do not apply that older claim to the current online edition or treat it as blanket commercial/AI clearance.
- [BCcampus Foundations of Physics licence/about page](https://opentextbc.ca/foundationsofphysics/front-matter/about-bccampus-open-education/) — specific book page says Terrance Berg's book is CC BY 4.0 except where otherwise noted; its contents list electrostatic fields/forces, electric fields, current and circuits. Commercial use is allowed with attribution, subject to item-level exceptions and asset review.
- [Paul Garrett calculus site](https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~garrett/calculus/) — author states his site work is CC BY 3.0 Unported unless explicitly noted otherwise; page lists first-year calculus notes/course pages. Commercial use is allowed with attribution, subject to each linked item exception and currency review.
- [Virginia Tech repository record: Electromagnetics, Volume 1 (2018)](https://vtechworks.lib.vt.edu/items/6f75e22e-ea6a-48a3-b441-65ce5ebf5331) — official item record identifies Volume 1 and CC BY-SA 4.0; its metadata lists fields, steady current, magnetostatics and time-varying-field coverage. Commercial adaptation is allowed with attribution and ShareAlike; inspect assets for exceptions and do not imply endorsement.
- [OpenStax Principles of Economics 3e preface](https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-3e/pages/preface) — current 3e is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and says prior written permission is required before LLM training or ingestion into generative-AI offerings.
- [Official 2018 Principles of Economics 2e PDF](https://assets.openstax.org/oscms-prodcms/media/documents/Economics2e-OP_s2jF42u.pdf) — PDF text states CC BY 4.0. [Current 2e web index](https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-2e/pages/index) separately says generative-AI ingestion needs prior written permission and some visual/assessment components have NC-SA. Resolve edition/component/use before commercial ingestion; the older notice does not automatically clear all current web-page or third-party components.
- [OpenStax revision policy](https://help.openstax.org/s/article/When-do-you-publish-revisions) — current and previous editions can both be supported; older editions may be retired. Preserve the exact acquired file/version and its included terms.
- [MIT OCW 6.002 course](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007/) and [MIT OCW terms](https://ocw.mit.edu/pages/privacy-and-terms-of-use/) — item-level CC BY-NC-SA and exceptions require review; no bulk content clearance inferred.
- [Wits EBE Senate Rules PDF](https://www.wits.ac.za/media/wits-university/students/academic-matters/documents/EBE_eBook.pdf) — 2026 programme/year/course-code metadata source already referenced in the local code registry. No content reuse licence was captured.

## Equipment, image/document and adaptation data

- [UCI AI4I 2020](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/601/ai4i+2020) — official CC BY 4.0; explicitly synthetic predictive-maintenance data, not real motor/pump or learner data.
- [Paderborn University Bearing DataCenter](https://mb.uni-paderborn.de/en/kat/research/bearing-datacenter) — official experimental vibration/current bearing signals; page says CC BY-NC 4.0, cite as specified and contact authors for commercial use.
- [IBM PubLayNet repository](https://github.com/ibm-aur-nlp/PubLayNet) and [license](https://github.com/ibm-aur-nlp/PubLayNet/blob/master/LICENSE.md) — annotations under CDLA-Permissive-1.0; IBM does not own images, which are subject to [PMC Open Access Subset terms](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/tools/openftlist/). Assess each article's source licence.
- [Official DocVQA benchmark page](https://site.docvqa.org/datasets/docvqa) and [WACV paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2021/papers/Mathew_DocVQA_A_Dataset_for_VQA_on_Document_Images_WACV_2021_paper.pdf) — challenge portal gates downloads and points to download terms; no clear dataset licence was established from the public pages checked. Do not rely on a mirror-applied licence.
- [Riiid EdNet repository](https://github.com/riiid/ednet) — repository states CC BY-NC 4.0 for research purposes. Not permitted as ordinary commercial training data and not representative evidence for KAIRO.
- [Google FLEURS dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs) — card declares CC BY 4.0 and includes Afrikaans `af_za`; read speech is an evaluation reference, not noisy classroom performance proof.
- [UFS SASL HLT repository](https://github.com/ufs-za/human_language_technology_for_sasl) — repository states CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and requires a formal data-sharing agreement for commercialisation; it includes synthetic fingerspelling work, not validated spontaneous SASL translation.
