# Engineering role sources (checked 25 October 2026)

## Purpose and judging connection

This small research fixture supports **Impact** (help engineering learners find sourced pathways) and **Usability** (make the distinction between employer requirements, vacancy status and a KAIRO study idea visible). Learner need: connect study evidence to a real pathway without implying that a suggested project satisfies an employer's formal criteria. Observable acceptance: each requirement points to a dated, official page locator; each KAIRO suggestion names its matching requirement IDs and boundary; uncertain availability stays explicit.

## Sources reviewed

1. BMW Group South Africa, [Graduate Schemes & Recent Grad Jobs](https://www.bmwgroup.jobs/za/en/opportunities/graduate.html?country=ZA&jobType=GRADUATE_JOB), read 25 October 2026. It describes a 24-month graduate development programme and lists an engineering degree as eligible. Its filtered vacancy panel said no vacancies were found. Record availability is therefore `closed` for the observed filtered listing, not a prediction about later applications.
2. Siemens, [Siemens Graduate Program](https://www.siemens.com/en-us/company/jobs/growth-careers/siemens-graduate-program/), read 25 October 2026. It describes a global master's-level programme, including engineering and related disciplines, work experience during/between studies, and international experience. The page explains that positions vary by country and directs applicants to vacancies. It does not confirm a South African engineering opening, so status is `unknown`.
3. Eskom, [Careers](https://www.eskom.co.za/careers/), read 25 October 2026. The page showed a graduate-in-training role for business enablement, alongside professional engineering vacancies. Those listings do not evidence a graduate engineering pathway, so Eskom was excluded from the records rather than implying equivalence.

## Data and wording rules

- `requirements` are only claims attributable to the cited employer page and locator. Excerpts are short; the data file carries the page URL once per role.
- `suggestedProjects` are KAIRO-authored practice ideas. `requirementIds` show the ideas' intended relevance; they are not employer recommendations, credentials, evidence that a learner meets a requirement, or guarantees of hiring.
- `availability` is an observation of the page on the check date. `unknown` means the official page did not establish country-specific current availability; it is not interpreted as open.
- No deadline, future cohort, degree equivalence, academic mark, experience duration, eligibility determination or hiring outcome has been inferred.
- These are two illustrative pathway records, not an exhaustive engineering job feed. No applications, employer contact, partnership or scraping were performed.

## Remaining gaps

Recheck official career portals before a learner acts. The data does not verify individual eligibility, qualification accreditation, work authorization, application acceptance, or current vacancy state beyond the dated observation. Learner usability and whether the suggested projects help are untested. A runtime integration must map concrete learner artifacts to `missing`, `needs-review` or `explicitly linked`; absence of evidence is not proof of missing competence.
