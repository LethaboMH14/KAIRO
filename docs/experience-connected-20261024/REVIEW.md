# A connected KAIRO learning experience

24 October 2026. Implementation review by Codex, gpt-6, effort unknown. Selected C identity retained. This is a source and deterministic-test review; no browser, camera or device observation was performed.

## What the learner should experience

1. **Enter my space.** A judge signs into a fictional engineering or informatics account. The assigned programme, year and selected official module codes explain why particular modules and studios appear. Generic guests must not be shown another learner's private material.
2. **Bring and check material.** My material separates importing, reviewing extraction, shaping a lesson and opening it. Extracted words are not the same as a complete understanding of every figure, formula or table. Review missing content before relying on a lesson. Original uploads and editable lesson blocks serve different purposes.
3. **Work through a lesson.** Keep the source available, inspect citations, read without the avatar, edit the lesson, ask a focused question or request a cited draft translation. Changing a voice alone does not translate a lesson.
4. **Explore the relevant experiment.** Module activity links now carry the assigned lesson ID. Motor and pump links retain that context back to the lesson's working. Informatics students receive their request-flow activity rather than engineering links. Prepared simulations have their own cited teaching scope; uploading a file does not generate a validated simulation.
5. **Use KAIRO beside the task.** One primary companion offers Explain, Hint, Predict and Check using the selected component or pump task/readings. Detailed sources and prepared steps remain inspectable. Narration is deliberate, cancellable and shared with the lesson. An optional 3D KAIRO uses the existing rig; the portrait remains the lighter default.
6. **Keep working and choose a direction.** Notes, question attempts and simulation reports are evidence of activity, not a qualification or employer verification. A programme-related direction connects actual module codes to preparation questions and a small practical task. There is no invented readiness percentage, job guarantee or partner reward.

## Access and capability boundaries

The camera feature loads existing locally hosted MediaPipe hand tracking only after learner interaction. Closing the access dialog unmounts the camera panel; the tracking session has deterministic stop/dispose coverage. There is no recording, continuous SASL recognition, text-to-sign generation, camera-based assessment or microphone input in this interface. A link to a signing resource is not an integrated translator. Device and cloud voices have different capabilities. Cited text translation is explicitly requested through the lesson help panel and still needs language-quality review.

The hosting policy allows same-origin camera permission so the user can choose Start, and disables microphone permission. Verified local tracker blobs require narrowly allowed blob workers/script execution and fetching; script tags remain same-origin and inline JavaScript remains disallowed. This configuration and synthetic lifecycle checks do not establish real-device compatibility. The Cloud Run service remains IAM-private.

## Reference principles and their application

- [NotebookLM source discovery](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/notebooklm-discover-sources/): make source selection and grounding inspectable. KAIRO keeps this inspectability alongside an editable lesson and a bounded experiment; no claim of equivalent extraction coverage.
- [PhET implicit scaffolding research](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.6544): use constraints, cues and feedback to support exploration. KAIRO pairs a learner prediction with a reading and an explanation rather than continually talking over the experiment.
- [WAI modal dialog pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/): access controls need deliberate entry, dismissal and focus restoration.
- [MDN camera policy](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP/Reference/Headers/Permissions-Policy/camera) and [getUserMedia](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MediaDevices/getUserMedia): hosting policy and explicit browser permission are separate gates.
- [O*NET mechanical engineers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2141.00) and [systems analysts](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/15-1211.00): task references inform preparation questions. These are US occupational references, not current South African vacancies or hiring evidence.

## Next improvements, in dependency order

1. Human acceptance of the complete motor and pump paths using both synthetic accounts. Check portrait/3D fallback, narrow screens, keyboard focus, slow connections, denied camera and stopped narration. Browser observation is still restricted for agents.
2. Extraction review that accounts for each page/slide/sheet and separately reports text, images, tables and formulas. Add a consented multimodal evaluation corpus before claiming whole-document understanding.
3. Lesson-quality evaluation: a cited objective, prerequisite check, worked example, learner prediction, feedback, practice and transfer task. Track citation correctness separately from explanation quality and educational benefit.
4. Cross-device drafts with explicit conflict recovery and ownership tests; preserve unreadable local data rather than overwrite it.
5. South African role and funding records with source, checked date, eligibility, deadline/timezone and expiry states. Do not turn prepared examples into live offers.
6. Language evaluation with fluent reviewers; choose trained/pretrained signing work only after defining supported vocabulary, consent, dataset licence and evaluation protocol. Local hand landmarks are a foundation, not translation.
7. Institutional invitations, roles, educator review and sharing consent before portfolios are visible to employers. No automatic external submissions or reward/payment claims.

## Judging evidence

Product: connected source-to-working route, tested context preservation. Creativity: optional KAIRO beside an interactive engineering task. Usability: one next action, plain labels and contextual return links. Sustainability: reused provider, stateless UI packages and existing hosted foundation. Impact: a testable access-to-understanding proposition; no measured learning gains yet. Presentation: a coherent motor/pump story suitable for real app footage after human acceptance. Intelligence: transparent context-sensitive authored coaching plus existing consented model-backed lesson help; no claim that authored coaching is open-ended reasoning or that progress is a trained mastery model.
