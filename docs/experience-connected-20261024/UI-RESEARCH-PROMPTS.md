# Focused research requests for the whole learner experience

Give a research assistant REVIEW.md and CONTRACT.md from this folder plus docs/KAIRO-PRODUCT-BRIEF.md. Treat application status as dated evidence, not a request to invent features. Request official documentation, public product examples, dates and accessible links. For each recommendation return learner problem, interaction sequence, decision rationale, privacy boundary, implementation dependency, failure state, evaluation method and what should not be built yet.

1. **Entry and private learning space.** Compare real university LMS programme/module enrolment and guest upload flows. How should a fictional judge account demonstrate assigned modules without pretending institutional verification? Design invite, sign-in, wrong-account, expired-session and empty-space states with plain South African learner wording. Separate demo, verified affiliation and individual self-selection.
2. **Material and editorial control.** Compare source-grounded research tools and editable document/lesson products. Specify page/slide/sheet coverage, missing figures/formulas, extracted text versus original, editable headings/steps, citation preservation, version history, safe regeneration and cancel/resume. Evaluate our existing editor before proposing another framework.
3. **Lesson pedagogy.** Find evidence for a short objective → prerequisite → worked example → prediction → practice → transfer sequence. Show how the same motor principle becomes reading, labelled diagram and controlled experiment without unsupported learning-style classifications. Define a rubric for citation accuracy, completeness, answerability and feedback usefulness.
4. **Interactive studio and KAIRO.** Study PhET and engineering laboratory interfaces. Specify one pump investigation and one motor exploration with equipment, changeable values, observation, expected uncertainty and a safe hint ladder. Design what KAIRO says before, during and after a reading, when to remain quiet, and how to return to source material. Distinguish authored simulation behavior from model-generated explanation.
5. **Access and multilingual use.** Use browser, WAI and language-provider primary documentation. Compare caption, reading, speech, draft translation and camera controls. Define accessible keyboard/mobile sequences, permission denial and cleanup. Investigate SASL resources and models with dataset licences and measured vocabulary; never equate hand tracking with SASL translation.
6. **Evidence and roles.** Compare portfolios and skills profiles with public sharing controls. Connect a completed task to an inspectable report, assistance disclosure and related role task; no hiring percentage. Research South African entry roles and funding sources with expiry and provenance, separating public metadata from restricted content.
7. **Design and navigation.** Audit the selected C palette and existing Manrope using contrast, touch targets, focus order, progressive disclosure and loading/error/empty states. Propose one coherent material/lesson/studio/work/direction journey. Do not replace the identity with a generic dashboard. Identify what the learner needs to see while an import/model request is working, cancelled or failed.
8. **Performance and deployment.** Evaluate optional 3D avatar loading, model/worker asset caching, low-memory fallback and data use on a South African mobile connection. Propose measurable budgets and a test method; clearly label initial targets as hypotheses. Preserve private account boundaries and the current IAM restriction.

Finish with a ranked implementation table: smallest useful change, owned files/contracts, acceptance evidence, cloud/provider cost implication, and remaining uncertainty. Map each item to Product, Creativity, Usability, Sustainability, Impact, Presentation and Intelligence without claiming official scores.
