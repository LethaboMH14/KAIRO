# KAIRO wider pathway and opportunity audit

**Date:** 24 October 2026 · **Mode:** read-only repository audit · **Criteria:** Product, Impact, Sustainability, Intelligence.  
**Need:** learners may need a coherent route from their course and interests through practice/evidence to role preparation and current opportunities. Existing source→lesson→practice→save functionality is useful foundation, but an end-to-end employability or opportunity service is not present.

## Summary

KAIRO has real, bounded learner context and career-preparation surfaces in code. It is inaccurate to describe the whole agreed vision as only “source → lesson → practice.” The current product additionally includes (a) synthetic course/module profiles, (b) curriculum-relevant next actions and studio links, (c) persistent direction notes, (d) role preparation rubrics with hand-linked practice, and (e) applicant-authored CV drafting. However, those are not integrated into a verified profile-to-vacancy matching service: no learner skill is independently certified, no live jobs or NSFAS/bursary feed is connected, no application/reward/employer sharing exists, and no employment outcome is observed.

## Evidence pointers and precise acceptance limits

- **Current course/direction flow:** `studio/src/learning-flow/model.ts` defines stages My material → My lesson → Explore → My working → My direction and chooses a studio by module activity. `studio/src/learning-flow/RoleDirection.tsx` contains explicit statements that demos are not vacancies/assessments/job predictions, and that live jobs, bursary eligibility, deadlines, employer rewards and placements are disconnected. Sources include an O*NET reference with US-task-context caveat; study-to-task mapping is KAIRO-authored.
- **Role preparation boundary:** `docs/ROLE-PATHWAYS.md` explains the static source set, closed/unverified availability, self-selected weighted rubric, device-local persistence, no application/CV publication, and no independently verified evidence. `studio/src/pathways/RolePathways.tsx` says no application or upload runs there and live feeds/tools remain planned.
- **CV boundary:** `studio/src/cv-draft/model.ts` labels facts applicant-provided, excludes unconfirmed items, keeps target-role requirements out of CV facts, hardcodes `atsScore:null` and `independentlyVerified:false`. `CvWorkspace.tsx` says data stays in the tab and is not sent to employer or saved to profile; session export is user-controlled.
- **Opportunity research vs runtime:** `docs/workstreams/opportunity-evidence/README.md` is a dated 22 Oct 2026 snapshot. It marks FNB Graduate R46838 closed at 31 Aug 2026, other pages as unknown where appropriate, and project goals as proposals. `docs/account-wave-20261024/NEXT-PACKAGES.md` calls live opportunity freshness a future package with official sources, `checkedAt`, close/expiry and relevance. No live updater exists.
- **Evidence profile proposal:** `docs/profile-patterns-20261024/README.md` calls learner→selected evidence→consented employer view a proposal and says no employer view/sharing, credential, live opportunity source, legal review or learner/employer validation was implemented.

### What is connected vs what needs a concrete bridge

The current connected learner loop can carry a learner from selected modules/material into a lesson, prepared subject activity, saved practice and a direction note. A role-preparation page is also available as a separate surface and may link a replayable practice record, but the link is explicitly informational: it leaves evidence level unchanged. CV drafting is another separate tool based on applicant-entered facts. Thus there are useful adjacent pieces, but no single validated chain of **learner-selected role → sourced current requirement → learner-owned/reviewable skill evidence → gap-specific learning/practice → fresh opportunity/bursary → consented application/share → outcome**.

To close that wider product claim, the next acceptance work needs (1) learner-controlled role/funding interests, with no mandatory CV/account for ordinary learning; (2) rights-checked official opportunity registry including source locator, retrieval timestamp, close/expiry, eligibility and explicit “unknown”; (3) a freshness and withdrawal/closed-record policy; (4) explainable comparison of sourced requirements against learner-confirmed vs reviewer-verified evidence, with missing evidence distinct from no skill and no probability score; (5) source-linked gap practice that rejoins current course/material/progress path; (6) optional, scoped, revocable employer share/consent only if legal/security reviewed; (7) separate user-confirmed application/interview/employment outcomes and a credible comparison design before impact claims. No automated application, third-party scraping, employer access or new data collection should be inferred from this audit.

## Product / market implication

The job/bursary story is a **roadmap extension and unmet-needs hypothesis** that must appear alongside the current learning product in the canvas, not as shipped functionality. It may strengthen the learner’s long-term value proposition if KAIRO safely connects study evidence to opportunity discovery. It simultaneously introduces different buyers, freshness obligations, eligibility risk, privacy/consent, rights and outcome measurement. It cannot be used yet to support a current employer partnership, FNB relationship, placement outcome, job access advantage, or buyer willingness to pay. Treat educational usage and opportunity-service usage as separate validation cohorts and metrics.
