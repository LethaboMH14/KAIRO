## Why this structure

Google's official YouTube ABCD guidance recommends Attention, Branding, Connection and Direction; the short slot benefits from a recognizable learner question, early KAIRO naming, emotional relevance and a clear next-step value. This is general creative guidance, not an FNB rule ([Think with Google production guide](https://business.google.com/us/think/future-of-marketing/production-guide-youtube-ad-tips/), [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en-AU)). A useful product-story reference is Google's *Parisian Love*: the credited collaborator describes a human story told through product searches/results, illustrating how interface actions can carry emotion without a feature list ([SpecialGuest project account](https://www.specialguest.co/projects/parisian-love), [Google's original announcement](https://googleblog.blogspot.com/2010/02/love-and-super-bowl.html)). We relied on those descriptions and official guidance; we did not watch videos through the restricted browser.

## Production and rehearsal checklist

1. Ask organizer/portal to confirm whether noon or 13:30 is authoritative, the required aspect ratio and upload limits, whether entrant must be visibly on camera throughout, and whether generated narration/visuals are allowed.
2. Record the entrant's direct-to-camera hook and closing line in one calm take. Keep the opening identity clearly the entrant; one narrator is KAIRO throughout the product section.
3. Capture only the real supported source review, motor interaction, practice prompt and saved evidence. Keep the citation/source legible long enough to read; don't fake responsiveness or imply general upload support beyond supported formats.
4. Assemble one simple match transition: corner problems → cited source → motor consequence → practice/evidence. No employment/funding result, testimonial, predicted score or bank partnership implication.
5. Rehearse a natural timed read. The table allocates 11 seconds to the entrant and 9 to KAIRO, but does not measure speech rate or pauses. Keep the 20-second runtime while adjusting copy and visual holds after rehearsal. Existing mock narration is synthetic timing guidance only; no human listening/timing acceptance is claimed for this script.
6. Add captions matching the final audio, check spelling `KAIRO`, keep captions and source away from the motor control and face, and leave enough hold on final name/line to register.
7. Export to the exact portal specification once confirmed. Check duration is 20 seconds, opening and ending are not clipped, audio is intelligible on a phone speaker, and the upload preview plays end-to-end. Keep a local master and verify successful portal submission separately.
