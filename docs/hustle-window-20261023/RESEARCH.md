## Recommended ingredients (creative decision, not compulsory checklist)

1. **Hook:** one recognisable learner frustration in the first3–4seconds.
2. **Who/name:** KAIRO, an interactive learning companion; make the learner audience legible.
3. **Mechanism/proof:** reviewed material → explanation → supported motor interaction → practice/evidence. Show one example, don't list infrastructure.
4. **Benefit:** something the learner can understand, try and explain. No fake improved marks or testimonial.
5. **One future beat:** employer-visible evidence/proposed learning rewards labelled 'Next / proposed'. No live bank partnership, redeemable cash or job placement claimed.
6. **Memorable close:** KAIRO and one value line; don't introduce a fake QR/demo URL before one exists.
