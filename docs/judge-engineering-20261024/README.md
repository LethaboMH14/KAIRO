# Wits Mechanical Engineering curriculum source notes

This evidence package supports an illustrative third-year Mechanical Engineering learning space for 2026. It does not assert that a KAIRO user is enrolled at Wits.

## Primary sources and locators

- [Wits Mechanical Engineering course finder](https://www.wits.ac.za/course-finder/undergraduate/ebe/mechanical-engineering/): Curriculum section, “Third-year” list. It presents the Mechanical Engineering year-level sequence and identifies fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, mechatronics, mechanical design, investigation, and other modules. It does not list course codes or semester placements.
- [2026 EBE Senate Rules and Syllabuses](https://www.wits.ac.za/media/wits-university/students/academic-matters/documents/EBE_eBook.pdf): Mechanical Engineering branch, “Year of Study III” table (printed p. 52) verifies MECN3071A Fluid Mechanics II, MECN3072A Manufacturing Technology I and MECN3077A Thermodynamics II. The 2026 syllabus sections verify MECN3066A Mechatronics II, MECN3069A Engineering Tools II, MECN3079A Mechanical Engineering Investigation, and MECN3080A Mechanical Engineering Design. Relevant syllabus text is paraphrased in the JSON, not copied.

The 2026 source supports course codes and titles and the year grouping for the selected courses, while the public course finder is the readable cohort curriculum source. Semester remains unknown in this record. Do not infer registration, prerequisites, completion, grades, or prescribed learning tasks from these listings.

## KAIRO mappings

Motor and pump are existing KAIRO studio enrichment routes. Connecting them to mechatronics/design or fluid mechanics is our authored curriculum-to-activity mapping. Wits did not prescribe those KAIRO activities, and no claim is made that they substitute for a course laboratory, project, or assessment. Engineering investigation and computational modelling are presented only as contextual reading enrichment where a KAIRO activity does not implement those methods.
