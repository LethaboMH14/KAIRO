## The proposition judges should understand

**KAIRO helps an adult university/TVET learner turn difficult material into a source-linked lesson, work through it with Rain or a reading view, practise the idea, and keep evidence of what they actually did.** The differentiating mechanism is traceable interaction: choose a source claim, change a relevant variable or attempt an explanation, see the consequence, inspect the evidence, and retry with appropriate help. A lifelike teacher is valuable when it makes that mechanism clearer.

The [published hackathon brief](https://appoftheyear.co.za/hackathon/) explicitly includes academic persistence, connectivity and accessible learning. Do not pivot into fraud detection because a launch presentation discussed fraud. Commerce and opportunity remain staged extensions; they must not obscure the first educational proof.

## Stronger-than-minimum proof, without invented achievements

Proposed judge path: a public/authorized lesson opens → learner reviews an OCR correction → Rain reads the same cited revision → learner interrupts and asks for a smaller step → attempts a question → sees an explained consequence/feedback → saves working → reloads and resumes → switches to reading without losing the task. Add one meaningful variable change in a supported studio, not a montage of unfinished rooms.

An observable candidate for the memorable moment: change a delivery or sales quantity; predict closing stock; reconcile expected versus observed; inspect the exact supporting source. Do not silently equate a discrepancy with theft. If choosing economics or pump faults instead, the same teaching pattern needs a domain-reviewed invariant and evidence contract.

Keep a runnable public synthetic fallback, a labelled backup recording when later permitted, and a plain reading path. A recording is backup presentation material, not evidence that live behaviour works in that session. No hidden developer clicks, invented live API results or disguised mock outputs.

## Intelligence evidence ladder

1. Correct deterministic calculation and source validation: useful foundations, not learned prediction.
2. Rules adapt a smaller step from attempts/hints and explain why: transparent adaptation, not a trained model.
3. Model generates a source-conditioned explanation with validated anchors: inference, not proof of educational correctness.
4. Forecast predicts a defined outcome on held-out observations; compare to a simple baseline, report calibration/error and failure groups: only then claim measured prediction. With sparse pilot data label experimental or defer.

For future evaluation, define the target explicitly (for example, whether an unassisted answer on concept X is correct), split by learner to prevent leakage, retain an untouched evaluation set and compare Brier score/calibration if predicting probabilities. Do not infer a permanent ability or learning-style label. Do not optimize engagement at the expense of learning or exploit vulnerability. These are evaluation proposals, not existing trained features.

## Evidence record and completion gate

Each change/handoff includes: criterion + subquestion; learner/buyer need; supported capability; test/measurement; artifact path/source date; actual result; limitation; owner; next acceptance. Distinguish proposed, implemented, locally checked, device-validated and pilot-validated. A task may be complete while a criterion remains open.

Only tick an item after its specific evidence exists. Before a competition freeze: no unresolved critical journey bug, source/cost/privacy claims checked, permitted licensed demo material, honest offline/voice/language labels, reviewer access tested under an authorized route, and known limitations in the presentation. Green CI is one input, not deployment or release approval.
