# Acceptance checks the user (or a stranger) has to run

Automated checks (duration, frames, codecs, decode, black frames, clipping, loudness, final hold) are in
`design/kairo-ad-20261024/qa/validation.json`. They prove the file plays as specified. They do **not** prove that
people understand or like it. These checks are human and remain open.

## 1. One viewing, one question (stranger test)

Show the master cut once, with sound, on a phone held at arm's length. Do not explain anything first. Then ask:

> After one viewing, can you say **who KAIRO helps**, **what they do with it**, and **why it is useful**?

Record the answer verbatim. Count it as understood only if all three parts are present in the person's own words
(for example: “students… bring their notes, check the source, explore a motor and practise… so they can show
their work”). Repeat with at least three people who have not seen KAIRO. Note anything they misread (for
example thinking the interface is generated, or that KAIRO gives marks).

## 2. Phone-speaker legibility

- Play on a phone speaker at normal volume in a normal room. Are all five KAIRO lines intelligible on first hearing?
- Are the three student thoughts intelligible even where they overlap (1.1–2.9 s)?
- Is any music or effect louder than the voice at any moment?

## 3. Silent viewing

Play the silent-readable cut muted. Can the captions be read at phone size before they disappear? Is the
final lockup readable for long enough?

## 4. Honesty review (before sharing outside the team)

- The student is fictional and generated; the cut says “Dramatisation”. Is that acceptable for where it is shown?
- Product screens are reconstructed from real copy and real models, labelled “Interface reconstructed for
  illustration”. Replace them with real captures (CAPTURE-LIST.md) before calling anything app footage.
- The voice in v0.1 is a temporary local synthetic voice, not KAIRO's app voice and not a person.
- No grades, stars, mastery, certificates, jobs, funding outcomes, rewards or partnerships are shown or implied.
- “Record yourself” requirement: the entrant cut needs the entrant's own footage; eligibility of a fictional
  presenter is unconfirmed with the organisers.
