## Generated in this task

- **Score and sound effects:** synthesised from numbers in `audio/build_audio.py` (sine/partial synthesis,
  Karplus-Strong plucks, filtered seeded noise). No samples, loops or existing music. Original to this project.
- **KAIRO and motor renders:** Blender 4.5.14 LTS (GPL; tool) EEVEE renders of the project's own assets.
- **Typography and interface reconstruction:** Manrope (SIL OFL 1.1) typeset by the compositor; copy from the app.
- **Encoding/validation tools:** imageio-ffmpeg's FFmpeg 7.1 binary for encoding; ffprobe 9.0.2 from the
  gyan.dev essentials build (GPL; tool only), zip SHA-256 `60f467265b1e3123…`, kept in the ignored cache.
