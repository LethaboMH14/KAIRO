## The strategic choice

**One starting wedge:** adult engineering and computing/IS learners in a specific supported university/TVET learning task. **One product job:** understand and apply a hard concept from a checked course source, attempt it yourself and keep attributable working. **One demonstrable difference to validate:** linked source → corrected text → cited lesson → domain-checked interaction or accessible reading → real attempt → feedback → resume, instead of separate study websites and chat summaries.

Do not simultaneously sell six studios, guaranteed bursaries, job placement, enterprise HR, SASL translation and a generic R49 unlimited multimodal subscription. Keep those as staged, separately validated horizons, with a clear bridge back to the evidence-first learner task.

## No invented market size

**National contextual size** (DHET 2024) is not a pricing multiplier. Compute reachable first market bottom-up: a *named institution and module* → verified roster eligible adults → permissioned outreach → interested learners → completed tasks → retained use → buyer approval. Until inputs are observed, TAM/SAM/SOM are **unknown**, not 1.1m multiplied by R49. For modelled scenario cohorts 20–40 learners is a target, not an agreement. The reported SET share includes science and technology outside KAIRO's initially validated domain.

**First addressable outcome**: at least one learner independently completes a domain-checked task from an openly licensed or approved source, accurately cites the source and retrieves their record after reloading. A small descriptive pilot is not proof of broad causal grade improvement. For impact, use an equivalently demanding comparison and follow-up design reviewed by a learning specialist.

## Payer decision path

**Learner** experiences concept and access need → **tutor/lecturer** validates content and trust → **academic support/programme manager** decides cohort fit → **IT/security/privacy** checks tenancy, data and rights → **procurement and finance** reviews cost and contractual scope → **contract signatory** authorises a pilot. Sponsorship is another real contract path, not assumed FNB funding. Separate all decision makers; a learner's enthusiasm cannot authorize a university invoice.

For employees, additionally validate company training manuals' rights, HR reporting consent, role/task safety, onboarding, data location and SSO integration, with custom content creation quoted separately.

## Business risk sensitivity and falsification

1. **Price pressure:** ask neutral affordability and maximum price questions; compare free PhET, Gemini Notebook, tutor and existing campus LMS. If willingness to pay is below all-in service cost, narrow offer, seek funded cohort or use reading and on-device modalities rather than forcing higher price.
2. **Usage spikes:** 3× completed tasks at unchanged seat revenue and 8× narration plus 2× retry must visibly reduce contribution. Premium TTS could dominate model tokens; measure charged characters, failed attempts and replays.
3. **Institution sales latency:** no buyer means no ARR. Model procurement lead time, onboarding/renewal and legal/content authoring separately from variable inference.
4. **Real cost:** compute user consented usage and cloud meters: P50/P90/P99 paid task cost, provider outages/latency, media GB/region, retention, recovery and review time. Never infer cost from capacity quotas alone.
5. **Fair pilot:** interview actual learners and buyers; record competing preferences, refusal, confusion and non-return. Re-plan based on disconfirming findings.

## 2026 competition versus commercial launch

The organiser's generic awards FAQ and current hackathon page affirm app usability and access-to-higher-education framing; the user-supplied 2026 programme in `docs/app-flow-20261023/PROGRAMME.md` indicates a 26 October 2026 23:59 submission and 20s pitch. Verify the **actual portal and time zone** now; don't prioritise invented enterprise revenue claims over an accessible demonstrable learner journey.

Evidence gates are different: competition requires an actually accessible working demo and clear problem→result; commercial launch additionally requires buyer agreement, realistic usage economics, privacy/data rights and support capability. Neither demands pretending that unobserved market traction, partnerships or impact are present.
