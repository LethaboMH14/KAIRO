## 1. Plain-language narrative and first user proof

### Working problem hypothesis
An adult engineering or computing learner can have study material and AI help yet still be unable to connect a specific reviewed concept to a practical consequence, test it, explain their independent attempt or return to that working. Learning activities are often distributed among sources, search/chat, simulations, notes and quizzes. The **hypothesis** is that bringing the reviewed source, contextual explanation, meaningful variable/trace activity, attempted working and recovery into one accessible task may improve understanding and continuity.

This is NOT a measured South African prevalence claim. Determine frequency, severity, existing workaround, cost in minutes/rands/attempts, trust and reasons for abandoning study with actual consented adult learner observation before asserting them as facts. Do not claim KAIRO prevents dropout or creates employment.

### Problem→solution chain
| Actual learner statement to validate | Proposed causal bottleneck | KAIRO mechanism | Proof to gather | What would falsify it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| “I have material but this motor/pump concept still makes no sense.” | Source is abstract; change→consequence unclear | Inspect corrected source text, see cited explanation, manipulate one **validated** variable with reading fallback | Correctly answered held-out application task; exact cited source; observed confusion and recovery | Existing lecture/PhET + notes works as well/faster/cheaper |
| “AI explained it, but I can't tell whether it's in my textbook.” | Grounding, version and interpretation invisible | Review extracted source, link statement to exact revision/page and show uncertainty | Citation-click correctness checked by independent reviewer; adversarial OCR samples | Wrong citations or unreviewed source silently promoted |
| “I did a quiz, but don't know what I can actually do.” | Grading conflates guesses, hints and independence | Versioned attempt, hints, revisions and saved evidence | Actual first attempt/assistance/revision persisted; independent rubric review | Portfolio only rephrases AI output or invents competence |
| “I only have my phone and data runs out.” | Interaction too heavy/unavailable offline | Fast readable low-data route and usable save/resume | KB transferred, load time, successful retry/resume on allowed phone/network profiles | 3D/video is required or offline label is inaccurate |

**Narrative for cold audience (proposed):** “KAIRO helps a learner turn their *own checked study source* into an explanation they can inspect, a practical concept they can try, and evidence of the work they can return to. It is being designed to work as a reading experience too—so the 3D teacher is an aid, not a barrier.” Do not use this as proof that all the above already works.

**Cold-audience comprehension test:** show title + first screen to a new, consenting adult unfamiliar with KAIRO. Ask “Who is it for?”, “What can I do first?”, “What changes for me?”, “What do you think it does NOT do?” without explaining. Record actual participant count, first response, confusion, corrections and whether they can complete the real synthetic-source journey; no invented quotes or statistics.

**Evidence standards:** Never cite a percentage without its population/denominator and calendar period. Never convert education enrolment totals into priced TAM by multiplying by a subscription. Never describe “internet access” as fast, low-cost internet. Never present accessibility usage as disability prevalence. Include retrieval/check timestamp, URL, original issuer, whether a quote is first-party, contradictory sources and confidence. For PDFs cite page/table and inspect actual page before publication. Update research when materially newer public data appears.

## 3. Competitive alternative: the uncomfortable question

**Why would a learner choose KAIRO instead of a free/cheaper stack of existing tools?**

- **Google Gemini Notebook** (formerly NotebookLM; rebranded 16 Jul 2026): source Q&A, citations and learning resources. Official: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/notebooklm-gemini-notebook/ and https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16215270?hl=en. KAIRO cannot claim novel source grounding.
- **PhET**: research-based free interactive science/maths simulations and offline alternatives, https://phet.colorado.edu/en/offline-access and https://phet.colorado.edu/en/about. KAIRO cannot claim interactive simulations are new or automatically outperform PhET.
- **Khanmigo**: guided tutoring; vendor's public example prices $4/month individual, $44/year excluding sales tax, with availability/region restrictions to verify: https://www.khanmigo.ai/pricing. KAIRO cannot claim an AI tutor alone is differentiated.
- **Quizlet**: self-study/flashcard/practice tools; published upgrade page shows US annual-billed equivalent $2.99/month Plus and $3.75/month Plus Unlimited, subject to market/plan verification: https://quizlet.com/upgrade. These do not establish South African landed prices.
- **Current course notes, lecturer/tutor, WhatsApp peer study, YouTube and a general model chatbot** must be in the same counterfactual. Zero additional spend and no account change may beat KAIRO on speed/trust.

Use the *same openly licensed engineering or computing source and task* to benchmark: first-step clarity, source fidelity, actual retrieval/practice independence, meaningful simulation, saved evidence, resume, accessibility, KB downloaded, time-on-task, mistakes, failure recovery and incremental cost. Conduct live competitor tests only with appropriate access; mark documentation-only comparisons differently. If simpler alternatives meet the learner job better, remove friction or narrow the KAIRO claim; don't count features as value.

## 4. Two distinct gates: PR merge and public product/competition claims

Every change should carry a **Need→Mechanism→Observable proof→Outcome hypothesis→Cost and risk** chain. Use the actual product's seven judging criteria to choose what improves.

**Merge gate (scope-proportional):** current exact-head CI, independent diff review, correct contracts/security/privacy, scoped user/problem explanation, named acceptance evidence and regression tests. User-facing work additionally requires authorised actual-use visual/keyboard/device proof or must remain blocked. A pure backend helper can merge when its own gates pass, without pretending learner gains were demonstrated. A source citation or clean mock never substitutes for live evidence.

**Judge/demo/investment gate (product-level):** beyond code, provide permissioned real-user journey and cold-audience comprehension, legally licensed and allowed screenshot/recording, authentic device/network/performance, educator/domain correctness review, measured cost after credits, research on learner/buyer need and reported unknowns. Rehearse failed-state recovery; a recording is not proof of live launch. No fake FNB endorsements or job outcomes.

For every feature, demand a one-page claim card:

```text
Problem ID / exact adult user & situation:
Current workaround and cost/friction (measured or unknown):
Evidence (issuer, date, denominator, source URL):
Hypothesised cause / alternative explanations:
Behaviour changed by this PR / exact code+API route:
Learner task and expected observable result:
Real source and synthetic test artefact (licensed):
Automated test command/run URL/commit:
Actual-user or device observation (or pending):
Competitor/counterfactual using same task:
Security/accessibility/language/offline edges:
Runtime/usage variable cost and fallback:
Judging criteria/subquestion and exact evidence:
Counter-evidence / reviewer criticism and response:
Next task/owner; acceptance vs blocked:
```

**Do not waive** an affected high-severity error, broken source identity, privacy leak, failed relevant test, fabricated quote, contradictory education content, cost runaway or an unusable basic learner path. Do not block unrelated internal code on unrun national impact studies. Every rejection creates a reproducible issue and retest.

## 5. Real-world discovery and economics: evidence, not invented ARR

### Research units
1. Adult learner trying one difficult engineering/IS task, with own or authorised material; watch their work before selling the idea.
2. Tutor/lecturer/student-success/disability-support practitioner checking domain integrity and workflow/oversight.
3. Programme/faculty buyer or funder with procurement, security, data-rights and budget constraints.
4. Optional sponsor/workforce buyer for a *different* scoped product; do not mix corporate training opportunity with the university beachhead.

**Neutral discovery prompts:** “Show how you handled your last difficult course concept.” “What did you try first?” “When did you stop or switch tools?” “What could the existing tutor/YouTube/AI not answer?” “What did it cost in time, data, money or confidence?” “Which exact sentence in the source mattered?” “Would you trust the recorded attempt, and what would make you distrust it?” “What would make you choose your existing method instead?” “What would your institution need before paying?” “Who has the purchasing authority, and what is the procurement path?” Record refusals, non-use and rival preferences. No leading questions like “Wouldn't you love a 3D tutor?” Get consent and minimise retention.

### Business and metered unit economics
Existing `docs/business-canvas-20261024` has broader price hypotheses (R49 learner, R35/R59 institutional seat and R149 enterprise seat) and a full-platform indicative shared-cost envelope. **These are hypothetical, not validated prices, sales or invoices.** Existing `docs/canvas-cost-research-20261024/` models Google Gemini lesson costs and identifies premium Google TTS as a possible cost driver. Do not extrapolate from a historic model without verified current SKU, execution, retry and billing evidence.

Maintain conservative per-**completed learning journey** workload: review/extraction pages, failed/retried requests, input/output tokens, embedding indexing/deletion, streamed narration text, speech transcription, Firestore reads/writes/indexes, GCS retention and delivery, Cloud Run CPU and cold starts, inter-region egress, logs, moderation, support/educator review and USD→ZAR/VAT sensitivity. Include the cheaper reading route and worst-case retries/provider outage.

Current official Gemini **3.5 Flash-Lite Standard** table shows Global $0.30/1M input and $2.50/1M output; **Priority** table $0.54/$4.50; **Flex/Batch** $0.15/$1.25. These tiers are NOT interchangeable. Source, checked 25 Oct 2026: https://cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/generative-ai/pricing. Verify actual model/deployment region/serving tier with the configured account before using in budgets.

Scenarios are NOT forecasts: 100 / 1,000 / 10,000 active eligible learners; light/base/heavy usage, high-failure/retry, zero temporary credit, no assumed paid conversion. Compute cost/user, contribution, support burden, data cost and retention; segment payer and users. Reject pricing if stated price cannot cover realistic use/support, then explore caps, sponsor subsidy, offline/reading mode or narrower supported tasks. Don't spend money to “validate” unless budget/access explicitly permits it.

## 6. The overlooked failure modes and the test-first response

- **Source and truth:** scanned maths/tables, missing pages, OCR misread minus/decimal, conflicting books, fabricated citations, quoted instructions attempting to redirect the AI, stale revision after edit, private source passed to the wrong owner/provider.
- **Actual learning:** learner guessed, saw hints, copied generated answer, repeated trivial question, was shown incorrect formula, misleading mastery label, age/discipline mismatch, no delayed recall or transfer; keep assisted/independent states distinct.
- **First use and access:** learner cannot understand landing screen, account/permissions appear before trust, unfamiliar avatar distracts, 3D loads slowly, phone battery/storage limited, data disappears, no captions, reduced motion ignored, keyboard trap, code-switching/STT false confidence.
- **State/concurrency:** network disconnect, session expires, duplicate generation, two tabs edit same lesson, changed source between selection and model output, provider fallback without consent, save/export fails, cancel returns late results, outage loses prepared work.
- **Business and reputation:** real buyer unclear, learner cannot pay, institution requires LMS/SSO/POPIA review, data residency unclear, content copyright, competitor is free, unusable public judge access, reviewers cannot access protected Cloud Run, old programme deadline or sponsor permission misquoted, refunds/support exceed contribution.
- **Security/operations:** malicious ZIP/PDF, injection, logging secrets, auth bypass via guessable IDs, bounded scanning/deletion, broken backups, unbudgeted model/video/voice calls, retry bill shock, hallucinated “verified skills”, absence of rollback.

Maintain a risk matrix with reproducible **real** failing fixtures, severity/exposure, observed frequency or unknown, test owner, exact corrected commit and documented regression rerun. High-confidence product failure creates a specific issue; weak evidence creates a targeted research issue; no open-ended “research more” loops.

## 7. A deeper continuous loop that keeps thinking critical

Repeat during actual active sessions:

**Research → Define a falsifiable user job → Observe baseline in existing solution → Build narrow KAIRO improvement → Measure functioning/experience/cost → Ask an independent critic to break it → Repair/re-test → Accept/merge if scoped gates pass → Update evidence/claim registry → Reassess strategy and choose next constrained task.**

Cadence for an automated runner when deployed: check CI/PR transitions event-driven; recheck official fast-changing provider prices or opportunity deadlines before public use; review feature/proof mismatches each accepted wave; schedule monthly research-refresh for slower market statistics. Ordinary chats do not work unattended. Don't create tasks recursively without bounded novelty, cost and duplication checks.

**Anti-sycophancy checks:** independent reviewer must say what problem is still unproven, where competitor wins, what user would misunderstand, what could fail after credits, what evidence would disprove the pitch, what feature could be removed, and why the user should trust this particular claim. Record *negative* evidence. The coordinator should reject feature accumulation with no measurable learner benefit.

### Exact acceptance record each agent should leave
```text
WHAT CHANGED? one user-facing outcome + code paths
FOR WHOM? named persona/context; last real observation or 'unvalidated'
WHY? first-party research with exact date, denominator and opposing evidence
AGAINST WHAT? credible alternative, same user task and documented limits
TESTED? exact SHA, CI URL, actual browser/device/screenshot if relevant
DOES IT HELP? observed task success/error, not vanity click count
WHAT DOES IT COST? scenario and measured/invoiced components distinguished
IS IT SAFE? authorisation, privacy, source/copyright, no content claims beyond proof
WHAT WOULD FAIL? worst credible edge case and reproducer
WHICH JUDGING CRITERIA? evidence per relevant subquestion and unknowns
SHOULD WE KEEP IT? keep/fix/de-scope/research; proof and reason
NEXT? smallest unblocked task, approved owner and regression gate
```

KAIRO should be technically ambitious **and** intellectually humble: an investor, mentor or judge should see the exact mechanism, the real outcome, the uncertainty and the next measured milestone—not just a long feature list.
