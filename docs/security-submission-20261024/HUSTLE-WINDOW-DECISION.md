## Cinematic choices that help rather than compete

Start close on you and the material; low, restrained frustration in sound. Use one notification sound per corner concern, not forty competing popups. At “Meet KAIRO,” reduce visual noise and open space around the subject. Use a circular match transition from the selected motor diagram to the rotor; a small controlled push-in can suggest understanding becoming tangible. Slow down the key consequence enough to see it. At the end, let the final name remain still.

Retain the selected C palette and existing Manrope. Keep captions large, high contrast and away from source labels and controls; one sentence at a time. Music stays below the voice and should not be needed to understand the message. KAIRO's familiar voice should be consistent; your real voice anchors the struggle and the close. Avoid synthetic crying, fake rejection/acceptance emails, made-up marks or implied testimonials. Real people, music and product/asset rights need appropriate permission.

Corner material can allude to bursaries, funding or roles in a longer film. In this 20s cut, such popups would promise a broader product and compete with the proof. SASL should not appear as a solved interaction because the current feature is local hand tracking, not translation.
