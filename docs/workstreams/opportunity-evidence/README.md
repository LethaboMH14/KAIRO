# KAIRO role-family and work-experience evidence

Research checked on **22 October 2026**. The companion [evidence.json](evidence.json) records source URLs, source-to-claim mappings, dates, requisitions, status labels, eligibility limits, and conflicts in machine-readable form.

This is a dated research snapshot, not a live vacancy feed or a personal eligibility assessment. Employer requirements below are paraphrased from official employer pages. Portfolio projects and their numeric acceptance criteria are proposals, not employer requirements or completed work. “Unknown” means the official page did not establish whether applications remain open; an accessible page or Apply button is not proof of an open vacancy.

## Three role families

### Business and data analysis / data quality

Two Discovery examples show different levels and specialisms. The [Business Analyst advert](https://careers.discovery.co.za/job/Sandton-1-Discovery-Place-Business-Analyst-GP-2196/1447995633/) asks for matric and an Informatics or relevant degree, and describes requirements elicitation, process modelling, solution design, testing/UAT, documentation, stakeholder work and incident analysis. It lists 2–3 years of business analysis experience and 1 year of UI analyst experience. The [Junior Data Scientist talent pool advert](https://careers.discovery.co.za/job/Sandton-1-Discovery-Place-Talent-Pool-Junior-Data-Scientist-GP-2196/1109525301/) describes SQL, Python, NLP/LLM prototyping and evaluation; it asks for an Honours or Master's in a named or similar quantitative field, but says other qualifications may be considered with relevant experience. BCom Informatics is not named as an automatic equivalent for the data-science degree criterion.

Both pages were visible on the check date and displayed an Apply action, but neither showed a close date or clear open/closed status. Availability: **unknown**.

**Proposed portfolio project — KAIRO Opportunity Evidence Board.** Build a local demonstration over synthetic role records, with traceable requirements and explicit unknown states. Suggested measurable work: write a one-page problem statement, 6 user stories and 10 acceptance criteria; model one current/future process; define 8 fields with source lineage; create 20 synthetic records and 8 data-quality rules that catch 6 seeded defects; produce 5 SQL queries and a dashboard; write a memo separating supported claims from unknowns. These are project targets only.

Prepare with Microsoft's self-paced [Transact-SQL learning path](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/get-started-querying-with-transact-sql/) (listed as 5 hr 45 min) and the [official Python tutorial](https://docs.python.org/3/tutorial/index.html). The learning content is free to access; certification exams are separate.

### Software engineering and automation

The official [FNB Engineering & Technology Graduate Programme, requisition R46838](https://firstrand.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/FRB/job/FNB-Engineering---Technology-Graduate-Programme_R46838) describes full-time work on end-to-end technology solutions spanning design, development, testing, implementation and systems management. It asks for strong academic results and a transcript, final-year study in relevant university programmes—particularly technology or engineering—and coding aptitude plus analytical, conceptual and problem-solving skills. The source gives Randburg as the location and **31 August 2026** as the closing date, so this advert is **expired** on the check date. BCom Informatics is not expressly named in the retrieved qualification wording.

**Proposed portfolio project — Offline Source-to-Lesson Service.** Build a local prototype using synthetic learning notes, with source references attached to lesson sections and safe behavior when offline or when a request is malformed. Suggested measurable work: version one request/response schema; document 6 error cases; process 5 synthetic files; add at least 12 automated cases across valid input, malformed data, unsupported claims, restart and offline behavior; measure cold start and memory over 10 local runs and publish the method and raw results. These are project targets, not FNB hiring criteria or completed results.

Prepare with the free-to-read [Python tutorial](https://docs.python.org/3/tutorial/index.html) and the [Microsoft Learn Transact-SQL path](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/get-started-querying-with-transact-sql/). Neither resource implies a job or certification.

### Digital customer support and service operations

Duties include troubleshooting, explaining digital tools, helping users manage and secure accounts, and logging, tracking and resolving incidents. It asks for matric and at least one year of call-centre/customer-service experience, and lists communication, empathy, problem-solving, technical skills, resilience and time management. The page was dated 16 October 2026 and visible with Apply on the check date, but gave no closing date or clear open/closed status. Availability: **unknown**. A portfolio project does not replace its experience requirement.

**Proposed portfolio project — KAIRO Digital Support Case Simulator.** Create 12 synthetic cases across account access, document upload and audio playback; define expected safe resolution or escalation; write a playbook with privacy checks and escalation boundaries; run all cases and record first-step accuracy, correct escalation and simulated handling time; build a local outcome dashboard. Prepare with Microsoft's [42-minute beginner module on service delivery in Dynamics 365](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/get-started-with-dynamics-365-for-customer-service/). The learning module is self-paced; a product practice environment or certification may require separate access.
